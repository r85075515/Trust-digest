10:T5b5,Advanced Substrate Technologies, the joint venture between TOPPAN Holdings and Broadcom, has opened a Singapore manufacturing facility for high-end FC-BGA substrates used in AI chip production.
What happened
The joint venture — Advanced Substrate Technologies (AST), owned by TOPPAN Holdings Inc. and Broadcom Inc. — has opened a manufacturing facility in Singapore for high-end flip-chip ball grid array (FC-BGA) substrates. It is the first facility of its kind in Singapore and TOPPAN Group's first outside Japan. Located in the Jurong Lake District, the facility will complement TOPPAN's main production hub. The EE Times coverage frames the move within Singapore's push as chip competition expands beyond process scaling into photonics, power devices, advanced packaging, and heterogeneous integration.
Why it matters
FC-BGA substrates are the foundation that high-end AI chips sit on, and capacity has been a bottleneck. A new plant in Singapore diversifies advanced-packaging supply beyond the usual hubs and gives TOPPAN its first offshore manufacturing foothold.
Sources agree / disagree
EE Times and EE Times Asia report the same opening; EE Times additionally frames it within Singapore's broader chip research, startup, and manufacturing ecosystem. No conflicts appear in the snippets.
Still uncertain
