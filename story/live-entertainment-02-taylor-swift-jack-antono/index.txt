What happened
The 17th annual Hollywood Music in Media Awards were presented at the Avalon in Hollywood, California, where "I Knew It, I Knew You," written by Swift and Antonoff for Toy Story 5, won song in an animated film. Swift accepted her first HMMA in a prerecorded video. The same night she returned to the CMA Awards stage after a 13-year absence, performing a mashup of "Our Song" and "I Knew It, I Knew You," while the 60th CMA Awards announced their winners. Ludwig Göransson also won at the HMMA.

Why it matters
The HMMA is watched as an early indicator for the Oscars' music categories, so a first HMMA win plus a high-profile CMA return gives Swift momentum on two fronts at once — pop and country — as awards season opens.
