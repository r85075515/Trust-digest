What happened
Super El Nino weather brought record-breaking high tides up and down the California coast on Wednesday, sending seawater into beachside communities and flooding roads and businesses. Beaches closed as Los Angeles and La Jolla saw all-time high tides, and big waves battered the tip of a landmark pier in Pacifica, south of San Francisco. In Sunset Beach, Lara Mitry had prepared to host her first Thanksgiving in a beachside home she recently purchased before the tides intervened. Inside Climate News warns the inundation could be worse in December. Separately, the BBC reports that storms on both US coasts brought wind gusts, heavy rain and flooding, leaving almost one million customers without power on Thursday evening and delaying flights.

Why it matters
Thanksgiving week turned a holiday into a warning: the same overheated seas that lifted this week's tides can lift December's higher. For coastal residents, the question is no longer whether king tides flood streets and businesses, but how often, and whether homes, piers and roads built for a calmer sea can keep up.
