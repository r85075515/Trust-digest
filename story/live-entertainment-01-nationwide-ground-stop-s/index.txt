President Donald Trump blamed the Biden administration for the FAA's aging system and urged Congress to approve more funding, while Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy pointed to a faulty router.
What happened
Trump broke his silence on the Friday ground stop, blaming the Biden administration for an aging FAA system and pressing Congress for more money, according to CNBC. Duffy offered a different explanation — a faulty router — leaving the administration with two competing accounts of the same failure, as TMZ's headline put it. Representative-elect Dan Koh, a Massachusetts Democrat, answered on MS NOW's 'The Weekend' on Sunday, blaming Trump for blocking more than $1 trillion in Biden-era infrastructure and FAA funding and tying that block to Friday's disruption.
Why it matters
The stop grounded flights across the country, so the question of who let the system rot is now a proxy fight over infrastructure money. Each side's explanation points to a different fix — replace the hardware, or restore the funding — and Congress is the audience for both.
Sources agree / disagree
CNBC, The Hill and TMZ agree a nationwide ground stop happened on Friday and that the explanations conflict; they disagree on who is at fault. Trump blames Biden, Duffy blames a router, and Koh blames Trump for blocking funding. Trust label: Accounts differ — Trump, Duffy and Koh offer three conflicting explanations for the same ground stop; the underlying cause is not yet established.
