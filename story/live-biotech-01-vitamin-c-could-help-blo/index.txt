What happened
ScienceDaily reports that researchers found vitamin C may block the formation of cancer-linked chemicals in the stomach after nitrate- and nitrite-containing foods are eaten. A new mathematical model suggests that taking vitamin C after meals could reduce these harmful reactions, offering a possible new strategy for lowering cancer risk.
Why it matters
Nitrates and nitrites are common in everyday foods, so a cheap, familiar compound that blunts their downside would be a practical win. The finding is early — a model, not a trial — but it points at a simple intervention worth testing in people.
