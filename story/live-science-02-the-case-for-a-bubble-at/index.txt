What happened
The explainer, part of Quanta Magazine's Origins of Life Week, lays out the case for a bubble at life's start. At its core, every cell is just a bubble of chemistry sealed inside a membrane. Why it matters
How life began is one of science's biggest unknowns, and the bubble hypothesis gives it a concrete shape. If the first cells were chemical bubbles, researchers have a physical starting point to test — what the bubbles were made of, how they held together, and how chemistry inside them became biology. An explainer that makes that picture clear is worth reading even before any new evidence arrives.
