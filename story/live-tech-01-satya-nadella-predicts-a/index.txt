What happened
Nadella says AI agents could upend the advertising-driven models that have powered the web for two decades, forcing companies such as Amazon to rethink how they compete for customers. The feed item does not say where or when Nadella made the remarks — no conference, interview or event is named — so the prediction itself is the whole story as reported.

Why it matters
The web economy runs on attention: advertisers pay to reach users, and platforms compete to hold that attention. If AI agents start comparing prices, placing orders and making choices on a user's behalf, the advertising layer in between loses value. Nadella's prediction points to a shift in where competition happens — from winning eyeballs to controlling the agent that acts for the customer — and that shift would reorder the advantages of today's biggest platforms.
