What happened
During the earnings call, Apple hinted at ways it could spur revenue through third-party opportunities, in the context of third-party AI apps. The material provided contains no further detail: no named product, no revenue model, no partner and no date. The headline's vagueness is the story here, because even the report itself frames the idea as something Apple may try to do, not as an announced plan.

Why it matters
Apple's hint matters because of where it points, not what it promises. If the company finds a way to earn money from AI apps it does not build itself, that would mark a shift from selling its own services to taking a cut of an ecosystem it hosts. Until Apple says how, investors and developers are guessing at a shape, not reacting to a strategy.
