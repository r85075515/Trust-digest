The knockout bracket for the 2026 NBA Cup was finalized following the conclusion of group-stage games on Friday, November 27, 2026. In the Eastern Conference quarterfinals, No. 3 Philadelphia visits No. 2 Atlanta on December 4 and No. 4 Boston travels to No. 1 Detroit on December 5; in the Western Conference, No. 4 San Antonio faces unbeaten No. 1 Dallas on December 4 and No. 3 Memphis plays at No. 2 Portland on December 5, ahead of the December 11 championship game at Hinkle Fieldhouse in Indianapolis.

Why it matters
The knockout stage delivers high-stakes single-elimination matchups featuring star-studded rosters—including LeBron James and Joel Embiid with the 76ers, Victor Wembanyama with the Spurs, and Cooper Flagg with the 4-0 Mavericks—while Detroit and Philadelphia reach the Cup knockout rounds for the first time. Beyond the trophy, players on the championship team earn USD 604,985 each.

Source differences

PhillyVoice published an independent team-focused report titled "Sixers to face Atlanta Hawks in 2026 NBA Cup quarterfinals," highlighting Philadelphia's first-ever Cup knockout berth after clinching East Group B with a 111-87 win over Indiana.

Still uncertain
Semifinal pairings depend on the single-elimination results on December 4 and 5, and player injury availability for next week's knockout games remains subject to team medical updates.
