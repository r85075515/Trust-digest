10:T5ac,Major US financial institutions are banning third-party bots from logging into customer accounts, and warning that any costly bot mistakes are strictly the customer's problem, according to Wired. The crackdown targets the AI agents people increasingly hand their banking logins to, letting software act on their money.
What happened
Wired reports that major US banks are blocking third-party bots from accessing customer accounts. The banks are also putting customers on notice: if an agent makes an expensive mistake, the loss is the customer's, not the bank's.
Why it matters
AI agents are only useful if they can act, and acting on money means logging in as you. Banks refusing that access, and disclaiming the fallout, forces a choice the industry has been avoiding: either agents get a sanctioned, limited way into accounts, or the consumer agent boom stops at the bank's front door. The liability warning matters as much as the ban, because it tells customers the convenience comes with the risk attached.
