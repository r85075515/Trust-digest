10:T58d,Taiwan's National Center of Photography and Images has opened 'Fashion in Flux' (時尚流聲：穿在身上的語言), an exhibition tracing Taiwan's fashion imagery through photography. The show treats clothing as a language and photography as the record of how that language changed.

What happened
The exhibition, reported by Vogue Taiwan, approaches Taiwan's fashion imagery from the intersection of photography and fashion. It organises the story around photographs, using the camera's view of clothing to trace how Taiwan's fashion image was formed and reshaped over time.

Why it matters
Fashion history is often told through garments alone, but this show argues the photograph is part of the story. Seeing fashion through the photographer's lens gives visitors a different way to understand what Taiwanese style meant at different moments. The centre presents the show as a photographic survey, not a garment retrospective.
