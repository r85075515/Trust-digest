On Friday night, November 27, 2026, at Scotiabank Arena, the Toronto Raptors defeated the Brooklyn Nets 124–100 in their final NBA Cup group-stage contest. Scottie Barnes (Scottie Barnes) led all scorers with 34 points, while Immanuel Quickley (Immanuel Quickley) recorded 24 points, 14 assists, and 10 rebounds with just one turnover—marking his first triple-double in four seasons with Toronto, highlighted by a 16-point third quarter featuring three three-pointers in just over a minute. Despite finishing East Group A at 3–1, the Raptors fell short on the point-differential tiebreaker for a knockout-round berth. For Brooklyn, Julius Randle (Julius Randle) and Mikel Brown Jr. (Mikel Brown Jr.) scored 21 points apiece.
