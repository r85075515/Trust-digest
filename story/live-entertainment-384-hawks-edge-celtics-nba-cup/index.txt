On Nov. 27, 2026, the Atlanta Hawks defeated the Boston Celtics 87-86 in Boston in the final NBA Cup group-stage game for both teams. Trailing 65-50 in the third quarter, Atlanta closed the period on a 20-4 run to take its first lead and held Boston to 35 second-half points. Jalen Johnson led Atlanta with 24 points, while Nickeil Alexander-Walker scored 20 points including the Hawks' final four. Boston shot 41% and committed 26 turnovers, capped by Jayson Tatum losing the ball with 3.8 seconds remaining after going scoreless in the fourth quarter.

The loss prevented the Celtics (12-7) from winning their NBA Cup group and extended Boston's slump to five defeats in its last six games.
