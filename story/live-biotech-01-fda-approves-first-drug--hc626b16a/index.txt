FDA Approves First Drug Shown to Help Patients with Infantile-Onset Niemann-Pick Disease Type C Live Longer. The approval culminates a winding development journey for Infilbray, and provides Sanofi with a new source of income ahead of Dupixent&rsquo;s looming patent expiration. FDA today approved Infilbray (venglustat) tablets, the first treatment for the neurological symptoms of Type 3 Gaucher disease (GD3) in adults and pediatric patients weighing at least 15 kg (approximately 33 pounds). FDA approved Lembaz (adrabetadex) injection, the first treatment shown to help people with a severe form of Niemann-Pick disease type C (NPC) live longer. FDA today approved Sunharbo (asundexian) 50 mg tablets, the first factor XIa (FXIa) inhibitor for use in combination with antiplatelet therapy to reduce the risk of stroke in adults following a recent mild-to-moderate non-cardioembolic ischemic stroke or a high-risk transient ischemic attack.
