What happened
EE Times reported that the AI boom continues to rewrite the rules of the game in the memory industry. Over the past year, major memory makers such as Samsung, SK hynix and Micron have continued shifting capacity and capital spending toward high-bandwidth memory (HBM) and advanced server memory, crowding out mature and specialty products. At the same time, the growing scale of AI models and inference demand is reshaping requirements. Against that backdrop, AI-driven HBM demand is boosting mature flash products as Macronix expands its flash portfolio for AI workloads, per the headline and summary.
Why it matters
When the largest makers chase HBM, supply of mature flash tightens and specialists like Macronix gain room — a second-order effect of the AI build-out that affects pricing across electronics.
