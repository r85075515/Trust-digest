10:T74d,What happened
Firefighters from the Scottsdale and Phoenix fire departments initiated a joint search-and-rescue operation on the afternoon of November 27 after a 92-year-old female hiker became stranded on Scottsdale's Pima Dynamite Trail. Scottsdale Fire deployed two mountain bikes alongside additional personnel from both departments to navigate the desert trail terrain and reach the woman. During the search, one firefighter sustained an ankle injury and received medical treatment at the trailhead.

Why it matters
High-age hiker rescues in the McDowell Sonoran Preserve and surrounding Scottsdale trail systems require specialized rapid-mobility units such as mountain bike teams to cover rugged desert paths faster than foot patrols. The multi-agency deployment highlights ongoing desert trail safety risks for older hikers even during milder late-November temperatures in metro Phoenix.
