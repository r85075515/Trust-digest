What happened
Christine Lagarde, President of the European Central Bank, said on the 29th that she will step down from the presidency early, Central News Agency reported from Frankfurt in a composite foreign report. The stated reason is that she wants to be free to speak publicly on important issues beyond monetary policy, and the headline states that she said she could no longer remain neutral. Why it matters
An early departure by the head of the European Central Bank is institutionally significant, because the presidency carries fixed expectations of neutrality on matters beyond monetary policy. The direct judgment is that Lagarde is choosing public voice over continued tenure, and that choice itself signals that the neutrality constraint had become incompatible with what she wants to say.
