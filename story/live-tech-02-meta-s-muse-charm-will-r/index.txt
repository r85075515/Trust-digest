10:T843,Meta plans to price its Muse Charm, a small gadget with a screen for chatting with its Muse AI agent, at $300, with a free Verizon 5G data plan included, according to reporting by The Information. Meta has not denied the report, saying only that it will have more to share soon about its upcoming AI wearable.

What happened
Meta is planning to price the Muse Charm at $300, according to The Information, as reported by TechCrunch, The Verge, and Engadget. Chief Executive Mark Zuckerberg revealed the gadget earlier this year and said the company expected to ship the Charm in time for the holidays in December, but he gave no price at the time. When he announced the Muse Charm, Zuckerberg said Meta's standalone AI gadget would not be cheap, but it might come with a free data plan. Engadget's report adds the carrier detail: the free plan would run on Verizon's 5G network.

Why it matters
The $300 figure is Meta's first pricing test for a standalone AI device, and the free data plan is the real strategy: a carrier subsidy that lowers the barrier to carrying an AI companion everywhere. If the bundle holds, Meta is betting that always-on connectivity, not hardware margins, is what makes a screen-based AI gadget stick. The December timing puts the Charm directly into the holiday shopping window, where a $300 gift with free service is an easier sell than a $300 gadget alone.

Sources agree and disagree
Three outlets, TechCrunch, The Verge, and Engadget, converge on the $300 price, and all trace it to The Information's reporting. No outlet contradicts the figure, and Meta's non-denial, that it will have more to share soon, leaves the report standing rather than rebutting it.

Still uncertain
The exact ship date, what the free Verizon plan covers, how long it lasts, and whether there are other prices or configurations are all unconfirmed. The $300 figure itself remains a report, not a Meta announcement, until the company confirms it.
