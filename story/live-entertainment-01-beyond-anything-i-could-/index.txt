10:T576,British DJ Carl Cox has been presented with his OBE by Prince William, calling the honour "beyond anything I could have imagined," in a ceremony recognising his services to music.

What happened
Cox, a pioneering British DJ and record producer, received the Officer of the Order of the British Empire honour from Prince William, who carried out the presentation on royal duties. Cox was one of several music figures named in the King's Birthday Honours List in June. Billboard's report carries his reaction to the award, using his long-running catchphrase "Oh yes, oh yes!"

Why it matters
The OBE puts a formal stamp on a career that helped take British dance music from clubs to the mainstream, and the royal presentation guarantees the story travels well beyond dance-music fans. For Cox, it is the highest public recognition of a career spanning decades.

Sources agree and disagree

Still uncertain
