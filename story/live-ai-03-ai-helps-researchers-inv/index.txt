10:T53f,Artificial intelligence is helping researchers investigate how thousands of medical studies share data and code, according to a report from MIT News.
What happened
The data and code behind a study reveal what was actually done and how. In a medical study, for example, they show how patients were selected for inclusion, how missing values were handled and how results were evaluated. Access to that data and code lets researchers answer those questions, examine the methods and build on the work, and AI is now helping to carry out that investigation across thousands of studies.
Why it matters
Whether a medical study can be trusted depends less on how polished its conclusions sound than on whether its methods survive scrutiny. AI that audits data and code sharing at scale gives research integrity an automatic check, helping readers and researchers judge more quickly whether a study is worth following up.
Sources agree / disagree

Still uncertain
