10:T55a,Google DeepMind researchers were able to formally verify that the widely used Signal Protocol encryption protocol works as intended, using a new AI tool. Their stretch goal is to do the same for 'all the software of the world.'
What happened
DeepMind researchers used a new AI tool to formally verify the Signal Protocol — the encryption protocol behind Signal and widely used in messaging apps — confirming it behaves as designed. The team says its ambition is to extend the same verification approach to all software.
Why it matters
Formal verification is the strongest guarantee in cryptography: a mathematical proof that a protocol does what it claims, rather than just testing. The Signal Protocol secures the messages of hundreds of millions of people, so a verified result matters beyond the lab. The takeaway is the method, not just the result — if an AI tool can verify protocols like this, software security verification could scale far beyond what human experts can do by hand.
