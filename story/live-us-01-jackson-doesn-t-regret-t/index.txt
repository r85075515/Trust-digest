Senate debates are usually exercises in restraint, so a “shut up” that the candidate refuses to walk back becomes the story — it signals how Jackson wants to be seen: unscripted and combative against an entrenched incumbent. The anti-data center protester is its own subplot: data-center politics are now disruptive enough to crash a Senate debate stage.
