What happened
Wired frames the moment as an arrival, not an experiment: ads in chatbots are here, and the question now is what works and what does not. The rest of the AI chatbot industry is moving to monetise through advertising, courting brands while trying not to alienate the people using the bots. Anthropic, by contrast, maintains its public commitment to keep its chatbot Claude free of ads.
Why it matters
Chatbots are expensive to run, and advertising is the oldest way to pay for a free service — but a chatbot is also a confidant, and an ad inside a conversation feels different from one on a web page. If rivals show ads can fund chatbots without driving users away, Anthropic's ad-free stance becomes a costly differentiator; if users rebel, the ad-free position becomes the selling point. Either way, the next year is a live test of the business model.
