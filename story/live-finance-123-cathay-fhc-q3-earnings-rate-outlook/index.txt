Cathay Financial Holding Co. (Cathay FHC) held its third-quarter investor conference on November 27, 2026. The group reported net profit after tax of NT$105.4 billion (EPS NT$6.94) for the first three quarters, with annualized ROE reaching 15.4%. Under IFRS 17 dividend rules combining net profit and realized FVOCI equity gains, cumulative adjusted earnings for the first 10 months reached NT$245.7 billion, or an adjusted EPS of NT$16.5. Cathay Life's net worth surpassed NT$1 trillion at the end of October.

Why it matters
General Manager Chang-Keng Lee (李長庚) stated that Taiwan remains one of the primary beneficiaries of global AI development, supporting supply chains and capital markets into 2027 despite a high comparison base. Meanwhile, Cathay United Bank Senior Vice President Po-Sheng Fu (傅伯昇) noted that solid domestic growth, global inflation, and Middle East conflict pressures leave room for Taiwan's central bank to raise rates by half a码 (12.5 basis points) before year-end alongside potential US rate pressure.

Source comparison
CNA, Economic Daily News (UDN), and TVBS News all covered the November 27 investor conference. TVBS detailed the IFRS 17 dividend calculation basis and historical payout ratio benchmarks (34%, 46%, and 53%), whereas CNA focused more closely on management's macroeconomic outlook regarding AI supply chains, bank liquidity coverage ratios (110%–120%), and year-end interest rate forecasts.

What remains uncertain
Cathay FHC has not yet finalized a specific cash dividend payout ratio for 2027, and actual year-end interest rate decisions remain at the discretion of Taiwan's central bank and the US Federal Reserve.
