10:T69f,Taiwan's Financial Supervisory Commission escalated its short-selling limits after AI-outlook worries sent US and Taiwan stocks sliding, with five stabilization measures taking effect on November 23.
What happened
Rising doubts about the AI industry's outlook hit all four major US indexes and AI-related stocks, and Taiwan stock-index futures and TSMC futures both fell in night trading. The FSC responded by tightening short-selling rules, including a full ban on short selling below the flat price from November 23, as one of five stabilization measures. Futures traders pointed to the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index, down 10.73% and closely linked to Taiwan's AI stocks, and to TSMC's American depositary receipts, down 9.48%, and warned of heavy systemic selling pressure at the November 23 open.
Why it matters
The measures are a circuit breaker for confidence, not for prices. A below-flat short-selling ban can slow a slide, but it cannot fix the AI demand doubts driving it. The test comes at the open: if the selling is broad and fundamental, five measures will look like a pause rather than a floor.
