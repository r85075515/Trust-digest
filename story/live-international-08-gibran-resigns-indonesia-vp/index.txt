10:Td92,Indonesia's Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka resigned on Thursday (Nov 12), personally delivering his letter to the leadership of the People's Consultative Assembly (MPR) in Jakarta, as a scandal over his education credentials closed in and parliament prepared to move against him. President Prabowo Subianto, appearing alongside Gibran at the Presidential Palace, said he had accepted the resignation with a 'heavy heart.' Gibran, 39, is the eldest son of former president Joko Widodo and had served two years of a five-year term. He is the first Indonesian vice president of the post-Suharto democratic era to resign mid-term, and the country's most senior figure to step down since Suharto himself in 1998.
What happened
The trigger was paper, not policy. Indonesian law requires a vice president to hold at least a senior-high-school qualification; Gibran qualified for the 2024 election with an equivalency certificate for his schooling in Singapore and Australia. On Oct 6 the Constitutional Court said the material on file could not convincingly prove an equivalent qualification (dismissing the petition on standing grounds); on Oct 30 the Supreme Court invalidated the certificate outright. On Wednesday, 120 lawmakers from eight parties petitioned the House to begin removal proceedings, and a plenary session was set for Thursday afternoon. Gibran - who had already offered Prabowo his resignation on Tuesday - met MPR leaders for about 20 minutes on Thursday and handed over a five-paragraph letter saying he acted 'for the interests of the nation and the state' and to keep the government running smoothly; it gave no detailed reasons. His resignation automatically halts the impeachment track.
Why it matters
Gibran was never just a vice president: he was the living link of the alliance between Prabowo and Jokowi, whose popularity delivered the 2024 ticket - a ticket Gibran could only join because a controversial Constitutional Court ruling carved an age exception for young regional leaders. His fall therefore tests the ruling coalition itself. Jokowi publicly reaffirmed support for Prabowo's government on Thursday, which may contain the damage, but the episode leaves a paper trail of courts, election officials and lawmakers all having passed a candidate whose basic qualification is now officially in doubt. Under the constitution there is no new election: the MPR must choose a replacement within 60 days from two names Prabowo nominates. The takeaway: Prabowo loses his bridge to the Jokowi machine at the exact moment his government faces economic and corruption discontent - who he nominates will say whether the alliance survives its own scandal.
Sources agree / disagree
AP, Reuters, Kyodo and Jiji agree on the core facts: the Thursday delivery of the letter, Prabowo's 'heavy heart' acceptance, the October court rulings and the 60-day replacement rule. Framing differs on motive - the letter and senior law minister Yusril Ihza Mahendra cite personal reasons and public sentiment; most reporting treats the impeachment petition and the invalidated certificate as the operative cause. Trust label: Multi-source agreement.
Still uncertain

引信是文件，不是政策。印尼法律要求副總統至少具備高中學歷，吉布蘭以他在新加坡與澳洲求學的「同等學歷證明」通過 2024 年大選資格審查。憲法法院 10 月 6 日指出，現有材料不足以令人信服地證明其學歷等同高中（該案最終以訴訟資格為由未受理）；最高法院 10 月 30 日進一步直接裁定該證明無效並撤銷。週三，八個政黨共 120 名議員聯署請願，要求國會啟動罷免程序，全體會議原定週四下午討論。吉布蘭——他週二已先向普拉博沃口頭請辭——週四與人協領導層會面約 20 分鐘，遞交一封五段的辭職信，稱此決定是「為了國家和民族的利益」、確保政府順利運作，未說明具體理由。辭職使彈劾程序自動中止。
為什麼重要
吉布蘭不只是副總統：他是普拉博沃與佐科威聯盟的活連結——靠著佐科威的人氣才有 2024 年的勝選組合，而他本人能參選，是因為憲法法院當年一項爭議裁定為年輕地方首長開了年齡例外。他的倒下因此直接考驗執政聯盟本身。佐科威週四公開重申支持普拉博沃政府，或可止血；但整件事留下了一條難堪的紀錄：法院、選務機關與國會當年一路放行一位基本資格如今被官方打上問號的候選人。依憲法不會重選：人協須在 60 天內從普拉博沃提名的兩名人選中選出繼任者。結論是：普拉博沃在政府正因經濟與貪腐民怨承壓的時刻，失去了通往佐科威機器的大橋——他提名誰，將決定這個聯盟能不能熬過自己的醜聞。
