10:T813,What happened
Northwest Restaurants Inc. acquired 28 Taco Bell franchise locations in the Phoenix metropolitan area from Luihn VantEdge Partners, comprising 24 operating restaurants and four units under development. The transaction closed in early November 2026 and was publicly announced on November 25, 2026, with Citizens M&A Advisory serving as the exclusive sell-side advisor.

Why it matters
The transaction marks Northwest Restaurants Inc.'s entry into Arizona as a new state market and lifts its total restaurant portfolio past 260 locations across the Pacific Northwest, Mountain West, the Carolinas, and Arizona. For seller Luihn VantEdge Partners, led by Jody Luihn (喬迪・盧因), the divestiture trims its holdings while leaving it with more than 180 Taco Bell restaurants across Arizona and East Coast markets.

Source differences
QSR Web reported the initial announcement on November 25 under the headline "Taco Bell franchisee acquires 28 additional restaurants", quoting Citizens M&A Advisory Managing Director David Stiles (大衛・斯泰爾斯) on Taco Bell's value positioning. L'Express Franchise followed on November 26 under the title "Taco Bell Franchisee Acquires 28 Units in Phoenix" (article body heading "Taco Bell Franchisee Northwest Restaurants Acquires 28 Units in Phoenix, Crosses 260 Locations"), citing QSR Web and noting that financial terms were not publicly disclosed. Hoodline published "Northwest Restaurants Buys 24 Phoenix Taco Bells" on November 26, adding historical context on buyer founders Sam Sibert (山姆・賽伯特), Brett Sibert (布雷特・賽伯特), and Adam Sibert (亞當・賽伯特). All three outlets trace back to the same transaction disclosure.

Still uncertain
The financial purchase price, specific street addresses of the 24 operating stores, and projected opening dates for the four under-development locations have not been publicly disclosed.
