10:T8e5,President Trump signed an executive order Friday evening to waive sanctions against Russia set to take effect soon under a recently signed legislative package. Trump says parts of the new law, which aims to punish Moscow for its war against Ukraine, are against US national interests.
What happened
The Hill reports that Trump signed an executive order Friday evening waiving Russian sanctions outlined in the Graham Act before they take effect. The president had officially signed the sanctions package into law last month — legislation posthumously dedicated to the late Senator Lindsey Graham, a Republican from South Carolina, designed to pressure the Kremlin. The Financial Times frames it as Trump exempting Russia from further sanctions mandated by the new law, while the BBC notes Trump waived several Russia sanctions as the law takes effect. Al Jazeera adds two threads: Trump waived more sanctions in a bid to ease energy prices, and gave Putin a reprieve on threatened energy measures after cutting a deal with Moscow to release diesel — as Trump's rift with Ukraine's Zelenskyy widens.
Why it matters
A president waiving sanctions from a law he himself signed last month is an extraordinary sequence: it converts a legislative pressure campaign on Moscow into an executive relief valve. The stated rationale — national interests and energy prices — collides with the law's original purpose of punishing Russia over Ukraine. And the timing, alongside a widening rift with Zelenskyy, hands Kyiv's critics and Moscow's spinners the same talking point: Washington's pressure on Russia is negotiable.
Sources agree / disagree
BBC World, the Financial Times, The Hill and Al Jazeera all report the same core action — Trump waiving Russia sanctions under the new law — with consistent framing around national interests and energy. Al Jazeera adds the diesel-deal and Zelenskyy-rift context; the outlets differ in emphasis, not in facts. No numeric or name conflicts in the snippets.
Still uncertain
The snippets do not specify which sanctions provisions were waived, how long the waiver lasts, or the terms of the reported diesel arrangement with Moscow.
