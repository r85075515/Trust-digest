Why it matters
Bond is one of the few franchises where the casting itself is the news — the choice sets the tone for the next decade of films. Picking Pattinson, a global star with both franchise and art-house credibility, signals Amazon MGM wants a Bond who can carry the series while Villeneuve rebuilds it. The takeaway: the lead is locked; the casting conversation now moves to the Bond girl and the rest of the ensemble.
