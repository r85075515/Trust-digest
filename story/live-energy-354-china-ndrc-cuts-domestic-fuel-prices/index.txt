10:T66b,What happened
China's National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC) issued an official notice cutting domestic refined oil prices effective at 24:00 on November 26, 2026. Standard gasoline prices were reduced by 440 yuan per metric ton and standard diesel prices by 425 yuan per metric ton, following a period in which international crude oil prices fluctuated downward after the November 12 adjustment before rebounding slightly.

Why it matters
The downward adjustment lowers fuel costs for road freight, logistics operators, and private motorists across China ahead of year-end transport demand. The NDRC instructed major state oil companies—PetroChina, Sinopec, and CNOOC—to organize production and transportation to ensure stable market supply and strict compliance with national pricing policies.

Source differences
The NDRC official announcement ("2026年11月26日国内成品油价格调整") provides the primary regulatory notice, stating the 440 yuan and 425 yuan per ton cuts, the 10-working-day international crude oil averaging mechanism, and market supervision instructions via the 12315 consumer complaint platform. Xinhua News Agency ("2026年11月26日国内成品油价格调整") reproduces the NDRC pricing decision verbatim for nationwide distribution.

Still uncertain
Whether the recent modest rebound in international crude oil prices will reverse this downward trend at the next scheduled 10-working-day pricing window remains uncertain.
