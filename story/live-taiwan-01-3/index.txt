What happened
Cho said the Longtan Science Park phase three project has been stalled for three years because of land expropriation issues. Hsu called on Cho to set aside political calculation, return to the Executive Yuan and get to work.
Why it matters
The judgment is that this is a paper-trail dispute, not a policy dispute: both sides agree the park is stalled, and they disagree only about whose desk the delay sits on.
