What happened
The show was the second night of the group's Asia tour and its first Taiwan stop in eight years, with Kaohsiung as the overseas opener. Fans who could not get tickets gathered outside the Kaohsiung World Games Main Stadium to listen in, and crowds stretched from the venue into the surrounding streets. After the show, five members went live eating late-night snacks: sushi that left the restaurant fielding calls into the early hours, and milk-soufflé bread that V turned into a 'nice' pun, with a Dandan Burger also spotted in the stream. V singled out fans in the livestream and was moved by a gesture from Taiwanese fans.
Why it matters
The numbers and the livestream together show how a BTS stop now works as two events at once: a stadium concert and a livestream that turns whatever the members eat into a sales rush for local shops. For Kaohsiung, the draw is a tourism showcase; for fans without tickets, the late-night streams are the part they can join.
Sources agree-disagree
ETtoday, Mirror Media, and Next Apple News all cover the same second night and the same livestream moments. The crowd figures differ in scope rather than in fact: ETtoday's headline counts a 105,000-strong crowd, while the feed summary counts more than 53,000 fans inside the venue.
