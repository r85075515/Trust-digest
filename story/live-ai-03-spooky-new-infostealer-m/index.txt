What happened

The data it collects spans login credentials and credit card numbers, Discord sessions, and UberEats receipts. Why it matters
The breadth of the collection is the concern the report raises. Login credentials and credit card numbers are the classic targets of infostealers, but adding Discord sessions and UberEats receipts widens the haul to communications access and daily-life records. The reported use of AI tools also matters because it is presented as the mechanism that lets the malware gather troves of data rather than isolated items. For anyone infected, the combined set of credentials, payment details, sessions, and receipts would describe far more of their life than any single category alone.
