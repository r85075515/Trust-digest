10:T678,Taiwan's stock index closed at 36,033.29 on November 20, down 2,393.6 points or 6.23%, the third-largest closing-point drop on record, CNA reported. Four leveraged Taiwan stock ETFs fell more than 13% — roughly twice the index decline — leaving investors with heavy losses.

What happened
Reporter Zeng Renkai writes that the Taiwan Stock Exchange weighted index fell 2,393.6 points in a single session to close at 36,033.29, a 6.23% drop that ranks as the third-biggest closing-point fall in the market's history. The damage was concentrated in leveraged products: four 2x leveraged Taiwan stock ETFs each fell at least 13%, about double the benchmark's slide.

Why it matters
A 6% single-day fall in a major Asian market is a shock in itself; the leveraged-ETF losses show how fast that shock multiplies for retail investors using geared products. A 2x fund is designed to double the daily move, so a historic down day becomes a double-digit hole in a portfolio overnight. The scale of the drop also signals how violently the AI-driven rally can reverse.

Sources agree-disagree

Still uncertain
