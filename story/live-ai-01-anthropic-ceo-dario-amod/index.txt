10:T84c,Anthropic chief executive Dario Amodei has publicly rejected the notion that humanity should hand governance over to superintelligent artificial intelligence, calling the idea "a bad idea" and pushing back on reporting that some inside the industry expect people to surrender power to advanced systems.

What happened
Amodei pushed back on an Axios report saying some employees believe humanity will hand power to superintelligent systems. Quoted in coverage carried by The Decoder, he said "turning over governance to AI is a bad idea." Axios reported that on Wednesday he rejected the notion that humans should surrender free will to AI, amid a growing ideological divide among top AI executives. He argued that "we should never sacrifice legitimacy or self-determination at the altar of" efficiency or capability, according to the Axios account excerpted in the feed.

Why it matters
These are described as Amodei's most significant public remarks since Anthropic went public last week, and they stake out a clear position in a live debate among leading AI figures over whether advanced systems should merely assist human institutions or eventually supersede them. Coming from the head of one of the largest AI developers, the comments frame AI governance as a question of democratic legitimacy, not just technical capability.

Sources agree / disagree
The Decoder and Axios both centre on the same rejection of AI governance and the same "bad idea" quotation. Axios adds the free-will framing and the context of an ideological divide among executives; The Decoder's headline focuses on the governance statement itself. No numeric or factual conflict appears in the available excerpts.

Still uncertain
The feed excerpts are truncated, including the closing part of Amodei's quoted argument, and the full Axios account of which employees or executives hold the opposing view is not reproduced here. The exact occasion and full text of the remarks are not given in the excerpts.
