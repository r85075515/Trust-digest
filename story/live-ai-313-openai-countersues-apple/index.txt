10:T732,What happened
On November 27, 2026, OpenAI filed a countersuit against Apple in California, accusing Apple of pressuring manufacturing suppliers to avoid working with OpenAI, stealing trade secrets, and enforcing one-year restrictions barring former Apple employees from recruiting former colleagues. The counterclaim responds to Apple's earlier lawsuit filed in July accusing OpenAI hardware personnel of improperly accessing confidential Apple engineering files.

Why it matters
As OpenAI moves from software models into consumer AI devices following its 6.4 billion USD hardware acquisition, access to contract manufacturers and veteran hardware engineers has become a decisive competitive bottleneck. A prolonged court battle through the scheduled July 19, 2027 trial could complicate supply chain partnerships for upcoming AI devices.

Source differences
TipRanks ("OpenAI Accuses Apple of Pressuring Suppliers to Avoid Working") detailed OpenAI's allegations regarding supplier pressure, one-year non-solicitation clauses, and Apple's prior denial of wrongdoing. Cryptopolitan ("OpenAI countersues Apple as fight over AI hardware suppliers") traced the timeline back to Apple's July complaint against OpenAI hardware executives and confirmed the July 19, 2027 trial date, while WTVB AM ("OpenAI countersues Apple for stealing trade secrets, FT reports") relayed Reuters and Financial Times reporting on the November 27 filing.

Still uncertain
OpenAI's allegations against Apple—and Apple's underlying claims against OpenAI—remain unproven allegations pending court adjudication, and the full unredacted counterclaim text has not yet been publicly released.
