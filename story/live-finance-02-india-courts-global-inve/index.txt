What happened
Bloomberg reports that India is courting global investment amid an escalating protest crackdown. Protesters climbed over barricades in central Delhi to demand the election chief resign over revised voter lists. The BBC reports that two top leaders of India's 'Cockroach' protest movement were detained after entering the barricaded protest site, and Bloomberg News reports the two were arrested and sent to jail for five days as the standoff with authorities intensified over the contentious voter-roll revision. The cluster also notes Bloomberg's 'Insight with Haslinda Amin,' a daily program of high-profile interviews on business, finance, politics, and culture.
Why it matters
The contrast is the story: an investment charm offensive running in parallel with a visible crackdown creates exactly the political-risk headline global investors read first. The voter-roll dispute behind the protests goes to the legitimacy of the electoral machinery itself, which is why the 'Cockroach' leaders' five-day jailing is drawing international coverage rather than staying a local policing story.
Sources agree / disagree
Bloomberg, BBC News-Asia, and Bloomberg News agree on the core facts — the barricaded protest, the demand for the election chief's resignation, and the five-day jailing of the two leaders. No conflicts appear in the snippets. Trust label: Multi-source agreement — three outlets agree on the crackdown and its trigger.
