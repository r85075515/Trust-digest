10:T53a,French renewable developers are rushing to lock in state-backed contracts before next year's presidential election, fearing a halt to new projects under a Marine Le Pen government, Bloomberg reports.
What happened
French renewable developers are rushing to lock in state-backed contracts before next year's presidential election. Their fear, as Bloomberg reports it, is that a government led by Marine Le Pen would halt new renewable projects, so developers are moving now to secure contracts while the current framework still applies.
Why it matters
The verdict is about political risk pricing: when developers expect a policy reversal after an election, the rational move is to lock in today's terms before they disappear. A rush of pre-election contracting also signals how much of France's renewable pipeline depends on state backing — and how quickly that pipeline could freeze if the political winds change.
