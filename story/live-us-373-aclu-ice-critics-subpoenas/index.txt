10:T676,What happened
On November 27, 2026, the American Civil Liberties Union (ACLU) sought a federal court order blocking the Trump administration from using dozens of grand jury subpoenas to identify social media users who anonymously criticize U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE). The legal challenge became public after Chief Judge James Boasberg (詹姆斯・博斯伯格) ordered the case unsealed on November 25, revealing subpoenas sent to platforms including Meta, Bluesky, and X.

Why it matters
The dispute tests First Amendment protections for anonymous online political speech and sets boundaries on whether federal prosecutors and Homeland Security investigators can use secret grand jury subpoenas without an underlying criminal investigation.

Still uncertain
