What happened
DigiTimes reports that Nvidia is paying in advance for capacity to support expansion in IC substrates, advanced packaging and testing, securing production capacity for the next three to five years. The push comes as accelerating custom ASIC projects from cloud service providers and rival chipmakers squeeze substrate and OSAT capacity. The feed text also mentions ongoing uncertainty over Nvidia's chief executive succession as part of the backdrop, without elaborating on it.

Why it matters
When AI demand surges, the bottleneck is often not chip design but the substrates and packaging capacity needed to turn designs into finished products. By prepaying, Nvidia buys certainty over its own supply for years ahead — and sends Taiwan's supply chain a dual signal of committed orders and funded expansion. For suppliers, prepayments change the economics of building new capacity; for rivals, they mean less spare capacity left to fight over.

Sources agree / disagree

Still uncertain
