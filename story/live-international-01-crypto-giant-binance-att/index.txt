What happened
Binance reported the breach on Thursday and said an engineer's laptop was compromised in a major security breach. Why it matters
The judgment is stark: a breach of this size at the largest exchange tests whether customer money can be recovered after it leaves the platform, not just whether systems can be defended. Recovery, attribution, and prevention now matter more than reassurance. If $6.3 billion can move in one incident, every other exchange faces the same question about its own controls.
