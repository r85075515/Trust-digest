What happened
Anthropic, the AI startup behind the Claude chatbot, began trading on the US stock market on Thursday and became one of the world's most valuable publicly traded businesses. The Guardian reports the closing price at just over $97 and a total market capitalisation of nearly $1.3 trillion. The Register reports that shares rocketed to $111.50 by the end of the initial auction, putting the company's valuation at around $2 trillion with a 34% pop in initial trading. The two figures reflect different moments: $2 trillion at the open, $1.3 trillion at the close.
Why it matters
A frontier AI lab listing at a trillion-dollar valuation means public markets are now pricing AI model companies directly, and investors are paying up front for a business model that is still maturing. The first-day surge also raises the bar: Anthropic will face heavy pressure to justify the valuation in future earnings.
Sources agree / disagree
The Guardian and The Register agree that Anthropic listed on Thursday and that its shares surged on the first day. Their valuation figures differ because they capture different trading moments: The Guardian's nearly $1.3 trillion is the closing market value, while The Register's roughly $2 trillion reflects the initial auction, so the two are not directly contradictory.
Still uncertain
The reports also mention a $1.75 trillion figure without fully explaining what it measures, and whether the stock can hold its first-day gains remains to be seen. Complete trading data will come from exchange records.
