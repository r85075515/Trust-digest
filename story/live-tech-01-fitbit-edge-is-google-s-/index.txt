What happened

Why it matters
The judgment is lineup logic: Google is segmenting wearables the way phone makers segment phones — screenless Air, mid-tier Edge, flagship Pixel Watch 5 — so every wrist budget has an answer. The replaceable battery is the sleeper feature in a market of sealed, disposable-feeling trackers.
