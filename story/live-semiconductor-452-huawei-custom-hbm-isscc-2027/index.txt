10:T655,What happened
Program schedules for the February 2027 International Solid-State Circuits Conference (ISSCC 2027) list a paper by Huawei's Shenzhen engineering team titled "A 64-Channel 4096-DQ Custom HBM with Memory Controllers and 5-Layer Wafer-on-Wafer Hybrid Bonding," detailing an in-house High Bandwidth Memory architecture.

Why it matters
Standard HBM chips from major memory suppliers feature a 1024-bit interface and face strict US export restrictions. Quadrupling the data interface width to 4096 data pins alongside integrated memory controllers and 5-layer wafer-on-wafer hybrid bonding signals a technical workaround aimed at sustaining memory bandwidth for domestic AI accelerators.

Source differences
EE Times ("Huawei's Next Big AI Move: HBM Workaround") analyzed the architectural trade-offs of the 64-channel, 4096-DQ interface and 5-layer hybrid bonding stack as a response to memory supply constraints; Venture Atlas ("ISSCC lists a Huawei custom HBM with 4,096 data pins") focused on the official ISSCC 2027 program listing by Huawei Investment & Holding Co., Ltd.; MLQ.ai ("Huawei readies a custom HBM chip with 4,096 data pins to take on") highlighted the full paper title and compared the wide-bus approach against standard HBM designs.

Still uncertain
Detailed bandwidth figures, power efficiency metrics, foundry manufacturing partners, and mass-production timelines remain undisclosed ahead of the February 2027 conference presentation.
