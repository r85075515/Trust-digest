10:T7b2,What happened
On November 27, 2026, Pratt & Whitney, an aerospace and defense business of RTX, announced the expansion of manufacturing capabilities at its Pratt & Whitney Kalisz facility in central Poland. According to company statements and Polish regional industry reports, the expansion includes investments in production technology, plant infrastructure, and the opening of a modern electroplating facility on site.

Why it matters
Pratt & Whitney Kalisz manufactures precision engine components supporting both commercial aviation programs and military aircraft platforms such as the F-16 and F-35. Expanding specialized surface-treatment and machining capacity in Poland helps alleviate global aerospace supply-chain bottlenecks and deepens Central European defense-industrial integration.

Source differences
RTX's corporate announcement ("News | RTX's Pratt & Whitney expands aerospace manufacturing") officially confirmed the November 27, 2026 capability expansion in Kalisz, citing investments in technology, infrastructure, and workforce. Polish defense outlet MILMAG ("Pratt & Whitney rozszerza możliwości produkcji lotniczej w Kaliszu") and local portal wKaliszu ("Pratt & Whitney Kalisz otworzył nową galwanizernię - wKaliszu") reported on the November 27 opening of the modern electroplating facility in Kalisz, while Ukrainian defense outlet Militarnyi ("Pratt & Whitney, що зокрема робить двигуни до F-16 та F-35, розширила виробництво деталей у Польщі") highlighted the plant's role in supplying parts for F-16 and F-35 fighter engines.

Still uncertain
RTX and Pratt & Whitney did not disclose the exact capital expenditure figure, annual component output increase, or new headcount targets tied to the Kalisz expansion.
