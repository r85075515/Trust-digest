10:T633,Andy Taylor, the guitarist who helped define Duran Duran's sound, has died at 65 after a years-long battle with stage 4 prostate cancer.

What happened
The band announced on Wednesday, November 25, that Taylor had died two days earlier, on November 23. Taylor, a Rock & Roll Hall of Fame inductee who co-wrote 'Hungry Like the Wolf' and 'Rio', had been battling cancer for the last several years of his life. He missed the band's Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction because of stage 4 cancer, and had said a cancer drug extended his life by five more years. Bandmates Simon Le Bon, John Taylor, Nick Rhodes and Roger Taylor released a joint message remembering him.

Why it matters
Taylor's guitar carried some of the most recognisable riffs of the 1980s, and his death closes the story of the band's classic line-up. The tributes from his four bandmates, the men who played beside him, are the measure of what he meant to the group, more than any chart position.

Sources agree / disagree
Billboard, Rolling Stone, the BBC, Sky News, Just Jared and Hollywood Life agree on the death, his age, the November 23 date and the cause. The figures flagged in the raw feed, 8217 versus 38, are an ingest artefact, not a disagreement between outlets.

Still uncertain
The contents of the bandmates' joint message are not quoted in the snippets, and no funeral or memorial plans are reported. Readers should check the outlets for the full tributes.
