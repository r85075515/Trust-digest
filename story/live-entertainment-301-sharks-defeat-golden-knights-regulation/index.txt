10:T7e5,What happened
The San Jose Sharks defeated the Vegas Golden Knights 4-1 at SAP Center in San Jose on Friday, November 27, recording the franchise's first regulation win over Vegas in San Jose after the Golden Knights entered the game 16-0-3 there. Michael Misa (Michael Misa) opened the scoring in the second period, Adam Gaudette (Adam Gaudette) tipped in a goal at 5:22 of the third period for a 2-0 lead, Jack Eichel (Jack Eichel) answered for Vegas with 9:27 remaining, Zack Ostapchuk (Zack Ostapchuk) restored a two-goal cushion with 5:56 left, and Macklin Celebrini (Macklin Celebrini) added an empty-net goal with 2:11 remaining. Goaltender Yaroslav Askarov (Yaroslav Askarov) made 27 saves for San Jose, while Vegas goaltender Carter Hart (Carter Hart) stopped 29 shots.

Why it matters
The result ended an 11-game overall losing streak by the Sharks against the Golden Knights and broke a long-standing home-ice hex at SAP Center where Vegas had previously avoided regulation losses across 19 visits. For Vegas, the defeat marked its fifth loss in seven games ahead of a Saturday home game against Montreal.

Still uncertain
