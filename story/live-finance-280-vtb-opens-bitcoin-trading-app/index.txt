10:T733,「What happened」
 On Friday, November 27, 2026, state-backed Russian lender VTB began allowing brokerage clients holding qualified investor status to buy and sell Bitcoin (BTC) directly inside its VTB My Investments mobile application. The rollout followed one day after the Bank of Russia approved the mandatory knowledge test required for prospective cryptocurrency buyers.

「Why it matters」
 VTB is among the first major registered Russian financial operators—having received central bank operator status in early October—to bring direct spot Bitcoin access into a mainstream retail brokerage app, setting the pace ahead of December 1 crypto trading launches planned by Sberbank, SPB Exchange, and the Moscow Exchange.

「Source differences」
 Bitcoin Magazine published "Russia's VTB Opens Bitcoin Trading After Crypto Test Rules", highlighting the timing one day after the Bank of Russia set its crypto knowledge test and noting SPB Exchange's December 1 start date. TokenPost published "Russia's VTB Opens Bitcoin Trading for Qualified Investors", specifying that trading operates through the VTB My Investments app. Startup Fortune published "VTB opens bitcoin trading to qualified investors as Sberbank eyes December 1", emphasizing VTB's position as Russia's second-largest bank and outlining rival timelines for Sberbank, SPB Exchange, and Moscow Exchange.

「Still uncertain」
 Trading volumes, fee structures, and whether VTB will expand its in-app offering to Ether or stablecoins such as USDT alongside December 1 exchange launches have not yet been detailed.
