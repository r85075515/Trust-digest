What happened
Tesla has opened Cybercab pre-orders to select customers for personal use. The vehicle is a gilded, steering-wheel-free two-seater, and buyers will only be able to purchase one if they live in a city where Tesla currently operates its Robotaxi service. The $299 monthly fee drew a remark from Musk himself that it is "too high."
Why it matters
The offer is Tesla doubling — or tripling — down on its claims of an autonomous future, asking customers to pay a monthly fee for a car they cannot drive themselves and cannot even buy outside a handful of cities. Whether buyers accept those terms is a direct test of how much the autonomous promise is worth to the public.
