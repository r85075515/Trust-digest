It is the first approval in this drug class.
What happened
The FDA announced the approval of Sunharbo, a new oral anticoagulant that blocks factor XIa, for patients who recently had a mild-to-moderate ischemic stroke not caused by a heart clot, or a high-risk TIA. The drug is taken together with standard antiplatelet therapy.
Why it matters
Stroke prevention after a first event is one of the highest-stakes areas in medicine, and a new mechanism — blocking factor XIa rather than older clotting pathways — gives doctors another option for patients at high risk of a second stroke. The takeaway is that this is a first-in-class approval, not a me-too drug, so its safety profile in real-world use will be watched closely.
