10:T8d7,Amazon has cut the price of Apple's M5 MacBook Air for Thanksgiving, with the most affordable model reduced by $300 and several larger configurations reduced by $400, according to deal coverage from 9to5Toys.

What happened
9to5Toys reported that the most affordable M5 MacBook Air a buyer can get is now starting at under $1,000, delivering $300 in savings during Amazon's 2026 Thanksgiving sale, which the coverage places on Day 8 of its Black Friday Week event. Other configurations carry larger discounts: Amazon is offering the 15-inch MacBook Air with 16GB of RAM and the 1TB SSD at $400 off the list price, the 13-inch model is also described at $400 off, and an update added the 15-inch 24GB model at $400 off as well. Listed examples include a Midnight 15-inch 16GB/1TB model and a Silver 15-inch 24GB/1TB model, each at $400 off. The same coverage noted MacBook Neo models dropping lower at $100 off, starting from $599.

Why it matters
The distinction between the $300 headline saving and the $400 savings is configuration, not a single price for the whole range. Shoppers comparing models need to check screen size, memory and storage before assuming the larger discount applies to the entry model. The under-$1,000 starting price is the figure that applies to the most affordable version.

Sources agree / disagree
Both items in this cluster come from the same outlet, 9to5Toys, so this is not independent corroboration. Its two headlines use different figures — $300 off for the most affordable model and $400 off in the companion item — and the body text ties the $400 figure to specific 13-inch and 15-inch configurations. Read together, the figures describe different configurations rather than two prices for the identical machine, but the feed does not list every configuration's final price.
