The Star additionally contextualizes the incident alongside a concurrent breach affecting the Hong Kong Arts Festival 2027 website.

Authorities have not yet clarified whether stored payment card tokens, hashed passwords, or Hong Kong identity card numbers were accessed, nor have they attributed the attack to a specific threat actor.
