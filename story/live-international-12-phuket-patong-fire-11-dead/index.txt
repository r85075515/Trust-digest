The blaze broke out at about 3:17 a.m. local time in a building housing a vehicle rental business in a town area near Patong beach, on Phuket's western coast, and spread to several nearby buildings; firefighters took about two hours to bring it under control. Police said 15 of the injured were Thai nationals and one was a Myanmar national, and that no tourists were among those killed or injured.

What happened: The fire started inside the rental-business premises and moved through adjoining shophouses before crews contained it. The cause remained under investigation as of the Reuters report, and authorities had not released the victims' identities or said how many people were inside the buildings when the fire began.

Why it matters: Patong is one of Thailand's best-known beach resort areas, and a pre-dawn fire in a dense commercial row that kills 11 people - all of them, on the police account, local workers and residents rather than tourists - raises immediate questions about fire safety, exits and occupancy in the shophouse stock that houses much of the island's service workforce.
