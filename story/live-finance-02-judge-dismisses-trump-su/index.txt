What happened
The suit concerned a 2025 Wall Street Journal article about a birthday letter to sex offender Jeffrey Epstein; President Trump denied writing the letter. U.S. District Judge Darrin Gayles in Miami dismissed the suit with prejudice, ruling that the president did not prove the defendants made defamatory statements with actual malice as required by law. The claim was reported as a $10 billion lawsuit. One outlet noted it is the second time this year courts have blocked Trump's efforts to sue the paper over stories about his relationship with the disgraced financier.
Why it matters
Dismissal with prejudice ends this lawsuit rather than leaving it open to be refiled in the same form. The ruling turned on the actual malice requirement: the court found the president did not prove the defendants made defamatory statements with actual malice as required by law. The decision also marks, on one outlet's account, the second time this year that courts have blocked Trump's efforts to sue the paper over stories about his relationship with Epstein, giving the outcome a pattern beyond a single case.
