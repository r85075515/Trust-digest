The hotel, called Lakeshore Citywalk Alishan Beimen (煙波城行阿里山北門館), pairs Japanese-Western wooden design with a hundred-dish buffet and a sauna.

What happened
The property is the newest opening from Lakeshore Hotel Group's city-exploration brand, located a short walk from Beimen Station on the Alishan Forest Railway, a line more than a century old. It began taking guests on 13 November, with a grand opening set for 24 November. The hotel's draws, as listed in the report, are Japanese-Western wooden design, a buffet called the Monet Feast (莫內饗宴) serving a hundred dishes, and sauna facilities called the Qinye Lutang (沁野露庭). On foot, guests can reach Hinoki Village (檜意森活村) and other timber-culture sights in the old forestry district.

Why it matters
Chiayi is the gateway to Alishan, and a hotel steps from a heritage railway station turns the journey itself into part of the stay — guests arrive by the forest railway at the heart of the area's timber heritage. The buffet and sauna are the property's bid for travellers who want a base for the Hinoki Village and timber-culture sights rather than a night near the mountain alone. The opening gives visitors a new place to stay within walking distance of the station and the timber-culture sights, rather than a base farther from the old town.
