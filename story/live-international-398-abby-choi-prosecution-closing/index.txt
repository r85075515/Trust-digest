10:T735,What happened
On Nov. 27, 2026, the 47th day of proceedings in Hong Kong High Court case HCCC 198/2025, prosecutors finished their closing arguments in the murder trial of Abby Choi (蔡天鳳). Former husband Alex Kwong (鄺港智), former brother-in-law Anthony Kwong (鄺港傑), and former father-in-law Kwong Kau (鄺球) all deny the murder charge while admitting to preventing the lawful burial of a body.

Why it matters
Prosecutors laid out their core theory of motive and execution before defense closing arguments begin next Monday. The prosecution alleged that deteriorating relations over child-rearing and Choi's plan to sell the Kadoorie Hill apartment prompted a coordinated killing and dismemberment plot, and argued that Alex Kwong killing Choi according to plan is the only hypothesis explaining all evidence.

Source differences
Sing Tao USA ("蔡天鳳案控方結案陳詞 指被告購物理由牽強 關係惡劣成犯案動機") details the prosecution's critique of the defendants' explanations for buying chainsaws, hammers, and plastic tubs despite lacking basic furniture in the Lung Mei Village house. HK Court News ("蔡天鳳案｜控方結案陳詞：控方結案陳詞：蔡遇害有3個可能性 唯有鄺港智按計劃行事 才可解釋一切") highlights the prosecution's framing of three possible scenarios for Choi's death, arguing only Alex Kwong acting per plan fits every piece of evidence.

Still uncertain
All assertions regarding motive, in-vehicle assault, and each defendant's role represent prosecution submissions and remain unconfirmed by a verdict, as defense closing arguments and jury deliberations have yet to take place.
