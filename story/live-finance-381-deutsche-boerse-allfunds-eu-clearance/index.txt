10:T91b,What happened
On Nov. 27, 2026, the European Commission granted unconditional Phase I merger control clearance for Deutsche Börse Group's planned acquisition of Allfunds Group, following the joint acquisition agreement announced on Jan. 21, 2026. Regulators concluded that the deal does not raise competition concerns in fund distribution management or fund dealing services. Reuters reported the transaction size at approximately 5.35 billion EUR, while Deutsche Börse noted that Allfunds held 1.76 trillion EUR in assets under administration (AuA) as of Dec. 31, 2025, spanning more than 1,400 fund partners and over 900 distributors across 66 countries. The transaction has not yet closed and remains subject to additional regulatory clearances and sanction by the competent English court of a scheme of arrangement, with completion now expected in the first quarter of 2027.

Why it matters
Unconditional Phase I clearance removes a major regulatory hurdle for Deutsche Börse's pan-European fund infrastructure expansion without imposing remedies, advancing the deal toward its expected first-quarter 2027 closing.

Source differences
Deutsche Börse Group's official press release carries the title "Deutsche Börse Group Receives Unconditional Clearance of Allfunds Acquisition from European Commission" and sets out the Phase I clearance, Jan. 21, 2026 joint announcement reference, 1.76 trillion EUR AuA figure as of Dec. 31, 2025, network scale across over 1,400 fund partners and more than 900 distributors in 66 countries, and expected Q1 2027 completion alongside UK court scheme sanction and other approvals. Reuters publishes the report under the title "Deutsche Boerse wins EU antitrust approval for Allfunds acquisition" and reports the deal value at about 5.35 billion EUR along with the competition assessment on fund distribution management and fund dealing services.

Still uncertain
The exact timeline for remaining supervisory clearances, the scheduled date for English court sanction of the scheme of arrangement, and the precise closing date within the first quarter of 2027 remain unconfirmed.
