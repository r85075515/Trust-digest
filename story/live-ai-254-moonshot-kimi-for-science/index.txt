10:T6b8,「What happened」
On November 27, 2026, Moonshot AI (月之暗面) opened applications for Kimi for Science, a global research support initiative announced via its official Kimi channels. Selected researchers and scientific teams will receive up to 20,000 USD in complimentary Kimi API credits to support scientific workflows, with applications for the inaugural cohort due by December 8, 2026, ahead of future scheduled rounds.

「Why it matters」
Foundation model developers are increasingly subsidizing compute and API access for academic laboratories to accelerate AI-for-Science adoption in fields such as literature synthesis, code generation, and experimental data analysis, while building long-term developer ecosystems within research institutions.

「Source differences」
Moonshot AI's official Kimi announcement page, titled "Kimi for Science | More scientific ideas, made possible", states the December 8 deadline for the first cohort and specifies the maximum grant of up to 20,000 USD in Kimi API credits. Tech outlet Pandaily reports the rollout under "Moonshot AI Opens Applications for the Kimi for Science Research Program", while AICoder's report "Moonshot Launches Kimi for Science: Selected Research ..." notes that the 20,000 USD credit ceiling corresponds to roughly 7 billion tokens.

「Still uncertain」
Moonshot AI has not publicly disclosed the total number of research teams to be selected in the first cohort, specific evaluation criteria, or whether participating projects are required to share benchmark feedback or open-source outputs.
