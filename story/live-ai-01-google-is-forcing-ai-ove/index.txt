What happened
Wired reports that Google has bundled its AI-generated search summaries with the traditional search experience used in schools, leaving no simple way to separate the two. The result is anger among parents and educators who say the convenience comes at a cost to learning, and schools are now searching for a way out — workarounds or settings that restore plain search for students.
Why it matters
The verdict is about who controls the classroom default. When an AI summary sits on top of every search, students get answers before they practise finding them, and teachers lose the friction that used to force thinking. If schools cannot switch the feature off, the argument over AI in education moves from whether to use it to whether anyone gets a choice.
