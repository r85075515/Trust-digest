10:T73d,What happened
On November 26, 2026, China's National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) granted marketing approval for bepirovirsen sodium injection (brand name Hibsago), developed by GSK in collaboration with Ionis Pharmaceuticals. Indicated for use alongside nucleos(t)ide analogues (NAs), the therapy is cleared as a first-in-class functional cure for chronic hepatitis B (CHB) based on Phase III clinical trial data.

Why it matters
Standard chronic hepatitis B treatments suppress viral replication but typically require lifelong daily medication because viral surface antigens persist. By targeting viral RNA with a finite six-month antisense oligonucleotide regimen, bepirovirsen introduces a scalable functional-cure pathway in China, which carries one of the world's largest chronic hepatitis B patient populations.

Source differences
GSK's official press release ("Hibsago (bepirovirsen) approved in China as a first-in-class functional cure for chronic hepatitis B (CHB)") highlights Phase III efficacy results and its licensing collaboration with Ionis Pharmaceuticals. Xinhua ("乙肝功能性治愈新药贝普若韦生中国获批") confirms the November 26 NMPA approval for combination use with nucleos(t)ide analogues, while Pharmaphorum ("GSK picks up key Chinese approval for HBV drug Hibsago") and MLQ.ai ("China approves GSK's Hibsago, a six-month course aimed at curing hepatitis B") emphasize the six-month finite treatment duration and antisense oligonucleotide mechanism.

Still uncertain
Commercial launch pricing in China, National Reimbursement Drug List (NRDL) inclusion timelines, and post-approval real-world durability rates across patient subgroups remain to be determined.
