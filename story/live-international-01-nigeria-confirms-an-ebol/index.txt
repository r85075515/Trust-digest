10:T5fa,Nigeria has confirmed an Ebola case in its capital, Abuja, in a woman who arrived from Uganda and tested positive for the virus, the health ministry said. It is the latest country to report a case as the outbreak spreads across the region.
What happened
The case was detected in a woman who had travelled from Uganda to Abuja. According to the health ministry, she began showing symptoms on 7 October and was admitted to a hospital in Abuja four days later, where she was later diagnosed with Ebola. The Guardian's headline frames the development as the outbreak spreading into western Africa. Kenya confirmed its first case earlier this month and has since recorded nine infections, the feeds note.
Why it matters
Nigeria is Africa's most populous country, and a confirmed case in its capital raises the stakes of a regional outbreak already spreading beyond its origin. The response now depends on contact tracing and isolation around an imported case rather than local transmission.
Sources agree / disagree
NPR World, The Guardian World, and PBS NewsHour all report the same core facts: a woman arriving from Uganda tested positive in Abuja. The Guardian emphasizes the westward spread into western Africa. No numeric or name conflicts appear in the short feed snippets.
Still uncertain
The feed snippets do not say whether the woman had contact with known cases in Uganda, how many contacts are being traced, or whether there are additional suspected cases.
