What happened
Musk, who is CEO of both companies, says special committees within both Tesla and SpaceX have been formed to look at a possible mega-merger, and that there is "a lot to be gained" from the combination, though it is no sure thing. Headlines across outlets describe the same development: "Elon Musk considering Tesla-SpaceX merger" (BBC World) and "SpaceX and Tesla boards form special committees to review merger" (TechCrunch); MarketWatch notes Tesla's stock rose as Musk dropped new clues about the possible SpaceX merger. Separately, CNBC reports that Musk ordered a Starlink slowdown for Ukraine. The cluster also notes that Musk and President Donald Trump have both recently criticized Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, who has flagged disruptions in US intelligence sharing.
Why it matters
A Tesla-SpaceX combination would fuse the world's most valuable carmaker with the dominant launch and satellite operator — with obvious questions about governance, valuation, and Musk's concentration of power. The Starlink-Ukraine thread is the sharper near-term issue: a private individual controlling battlefield connectivity is already a geopolitical fact, and any slowdown order makes it a political one.
Sources agree / disagree
BBC World, TechCrunch, and MarketWatch agree on the merger committees; CNBC stands alone in the snippets on the Starlink slowdown report. The Zelenskyy-Trump criticism thread appears as context rather than a disputed claim. No numeric or name conflicts appear.
Still uncertain
