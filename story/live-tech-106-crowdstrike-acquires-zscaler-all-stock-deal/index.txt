Zscaler shareholders will receive 1.135 shares of CrowdStrike common stock for each Zscaler share held, representing an implied offer price of $260.41 per share and an equity value of roughly $42.46 billion. Completion is targeted for the second half of CrowdStrike's fiscal year 2028, subject to regulatory and shareholder approvals.

The acquisition pairs CrowdStrike's Falcon endpoint security platform with Zscaler's cloud-native zero-trust network security services, which process over 750 billion daily transactions across half of the Fortune 500. CrowdStrike CEO George Kurtz (喬治·庫爾茨) stated that the merger positions the combined firm to defend enterprises against AI-accelerated cyberattacks while consolidating vendor sprawl for corporate security teams.

Press disclosures from CrowdStrike and financial reports from International Finance, The Paypers, and Morningstar align on the 1.135 share exchange ratio, the $43.9 billion headline valuation, and the projected $10 billion combined annual recurring revenue at closing. Channel partners cited by Channel Dive noted strong strategic complementarity alongside potential integration complexity across overlapping enterprise modules.

Approval timelines from US and European antitrust regulators, as well as the final dollar value of the all-stock payout upon closing, remain subject to review and market fluctuations.
