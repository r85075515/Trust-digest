10:T7b7,發生了什麼事
Ahead of Taiwan's local elections on November 28, the Yilan District Prosecutors Office launched searches and summoned 13 individuals over suspected coordinated vote-buying across township mayor, township representative, and village chief races in Sanxing Township. Prosecutors seized cash and notebooks during the raids. Following questioning, Kuomintang township mayoral nominee and incumbent Acting Mayor Chiang Chun-ming (江淳銘) and a township representative candidate surnamed Wu were each ordered released on NT$150,000 bail, while a Guilin Village chief candidate surnamed Wu was released on NT$100,000 bail for alleged violations of the Public Officials Election and Recall Act, which have not yet been determined by a court. One campaign broker was released on NT$300,000 bail, and prosecutors requested incommunicado detention for three other brokers.

為什麼重要
Coordinated multi-tier vote-buying involves local brokers simultaneously soliciting votes for candidates across different municipal offices, amplifying illicit influence over grassroots elections. Rapid prosecutorial action on the eve of polling underscores judicial enforcement against electoral bribery in Taiwan's local government contests.

來源異同
Both the Central News Agency (CNA) and United Daily News (UDN) report identical figures regarding the 13 individuals summoned, the bail amounts of NT$150,000 for Chiang Chun-ming and the township representative candidate, NT$100,000 for the village chief candidate, NT$300,000 for one broker, and detention requests for three other brokers.

仍不確定
Whether the court will approve the incommunicado detention requests for the three campaign brokers, the exact cash amounts seized, and whether prosecutors will file formal indictments after further investigation remain pending.
