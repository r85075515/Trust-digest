Under the protocol, if waiting times reach a red-light alert—estimated at 45 minutes to one hour—station staff regulate platform access in alternating cycles of 10 minutes open and 5 minutes paused, while adding 20 all-non-reserved trains across Friday evening and Saturday morning.

The measure marks an operational shift for Taiwan's primary north-south rail artery during peak holiday and election travel, aiming to prevent platform overcrowding in underground stations where vertical queuing space is constrained.
