10:T57a,Babcock International Group signed an £8.7 billion ($11.41 billion) Gateway Agreement with the U.K. Ministry of Defense (MOD) to sustain and maintain the Royal Navy's nuclear-powered submarine fleet, marking the largest contract in the company's history. Taking effect on December 1, 2026, with options for extension, the framework covers submarine maintenance, technical services, naval base operations, and a new Plymouth Capability Center expected to house up to 2,000 staff.

The agreement underpins Britain's continuous-at-sea nuclear deterrent and submarine availability while safeguarding 8,500 existing jobs and creating 3,000 new full-time positions alongside more than 2,500 apprenticeship and graduate roles. Following the announcement on November 26, Babcock shares rose roughly 6.55% to a three-month high in London trading.

Dow Jones (via Morningstar), Investing.com, and Naval Today all report the identical £8.7 billion valuation, December 1 effective date, and 3,000 new job creation figure. Defense Mirror adds details on the 8,500 safeguarded jobs and the Plymouth Capability Center capacity.

The exact multi-year duration before extension options are exercised and specific annual spending tranches under the framework have not been publicly itemized by the MOD.
