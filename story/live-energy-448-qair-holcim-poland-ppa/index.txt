10:T5f7,What happened
On November 26, 2026, in an announcement from Paris and Wrocław, renewable energy company Qair revealed that it has signed a 10-year power purchase agreement (PPA) with Holcim Polska S.A., a subsidiary of building materials group Holcim. Under the contract, Qair will develop a solar photovoltaic plant on land owned by Holcim Polska to supply 18 GWh of renewable electricity per year. The agreement combines a virtual PPA structure with on-site solar development to balance financial flexibility and operational resilience for Holcim's Polish operations.

Why it matters
Heavy building materials manufacturing is energy-intensive; pairing on-site land utilization with a decade-long renewable supply contract locks in clean power delivery while advancing industrial decarbonization in Poland.

Source differences
Qair Group published the corporate release "Qair signs long-term PPA with Holcim in Poland", while industry publication Modern Power Systems reported the same contract under "Qair signs PPA with Holcim Polska for 18 GWh of solar power". Both reports originate from Qair's November 26 press release and state the same 10-year duration and 18 GWh annual output.

Still uncertain
Neither company disclosed the solar plant's installed megawatt capacity, exact municipal site in Poland, grid connection schedule, or contracted electricity pricing terms.
