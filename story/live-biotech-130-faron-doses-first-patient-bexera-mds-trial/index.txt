10:T602,What happened
Faron Pharmaceuticals Ltd. announced on November 26, 2026, that the first patient has been dosed in its global Phase 2b BEXERA clinical trial. The patient received treatment at King's College Hospital in London, UK. The study evaluates Faron's wholly owned investigational immunotherapy bexmarilimab in combination with azacitidine for patients with previously untreated high-risk myelodysplastic syndromes (HR-MDS).

Why it matters
High-risk myelodysplastic syndromes are aggressive bone marrow disorders with limited frontline treatment options and a high risk of progression to acute myeloid leukemia. Bexmarilimab is designed to reprogram immunosuppressive macrophages in the tumor microenvironment, potentially enhancing response rates when paired with standard hypomethylating therapy.

Source comparison
BioSpace, OncoDaily, and BioStock all reported Faron's November 26 announcement of first-patient dosing in the Phase 2b BEXERA study. All three outlets aligned on the trial name, target indication of treatment-naive HR-MDS, and the dosing milestone, with OncoDaily specifically noting the treatment site at King's College Hospital in London.

What remains uncertain
Interim efficacy readout timelines, patient enrollment completion dates across global trial sites, and eventual Phase 3 registrational requirements remain subject to clinical recruitment pace.
