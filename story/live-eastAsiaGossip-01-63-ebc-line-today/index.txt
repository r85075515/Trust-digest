What happened
The Golden Horse executive committee announced the lineup on November 9. The headline is the gathering of four chairs: Chang, Lee, and Lee Ping-bing returning to back Chang Chen, who took over as chair. The actress lineup is equally stacked, with Shu Qi, Gwei Lun-mei, Lam, and Hsieh Ying-hsuan presenting alongside Lau. International guests add another layer: Oscar-winning director Bong Joon-ho, Japanese actor Sota Fukushi, Oscar-nominated actress Rinko Kikuchi, (G)I-dle member Shuhua, and Thai singer Yeh Chih-yu (葉芷妤) are all confirmed to attend.
Why it matters
The Golden Horse Awards are the most prestigious film honours in the Chinese-speaking world, and a presenter list like this is a statement of institutional continuity as much as star power. Bringing three former chairs back for the new chair's first ceremony signals a smooth handover, while the mix of veteran actresses and international names shows the awards still command the region's attention.
