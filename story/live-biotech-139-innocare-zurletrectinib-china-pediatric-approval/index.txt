Biopharmaceutical company InnoCare Pharma (諾誠健華) announced on November 27 that China's National Medical Products Administration (NMPA) has approved its next-generation pan-tropomyosin receptor kinase (TRK) inhibitor zurletrectinib (佐來曲替尼, ICP-723) for the treatment of pediatric patients aged 2 to under 12 years with locally advanced or metastatic solid tumors harboring neurotrophic tyrosine receptor kinase (NTRK) gene fusions. The pediatric label expansion follows the drug's earlier approval for adult and adolescent patients and its inclusion in China's National Reimbursement Drug List.

為什麼重要
NTRK gene fusions act as oncogenic drivers across diverse rare pediatric cancers, including infantile fibrosarcoma and pediatric gliomas. Next-generation TRK inhibitors are engineered to penetrate the blood-brain barrier and overcome acquired resistance mutations associated with first-generation therapies.

來源異同
Corporate releases carried via GlobeNewswire, BioSpace, Morningstar, and GeneOnline (基因線上) consistently report the November 27 NMPA approval of zurletrectinib for pediatric solid tumor patients in China. Because all reports stem from InnoCare's official disclosure, independent clinical commentary is limited.

仍不確定
Commercial launch pricing for the pediatric formulation, the timeline for extending the indication to infants under two years of age, and overseas regulatory filing schedules remain to be announced.
