What happened
NVIDIA announced the expanded CUDA-X portfolio, with chipmakers and equipment suppliers adopting GPU-accelerated libraries for simulation, inspection and process optimization. Pushing GPU-accelerated libraries down to atomic-level simulation inserts AI compute directly into the top of the chipmaking stack — whoever owns that toolchain owns the R&D velocity of the next process generation.
