Why it matters
Most AI perception work chases sharper vision, but sound carries information vision cannot — what happens out of frame, behind walls, or in the dark. A machine that truly understands sound, rather than merely recording it, would change how robots, assistants, and monitoring systems notice the world. That is the bet: hearing as a first-class sense for machines, not an accessory.
