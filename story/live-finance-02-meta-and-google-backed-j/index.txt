10:T574,Meta- and Google-backed Jio Platforms jumped more than 21% on its trading debut, capping India's largest-ever IPO — and instantly becoming the country's second-most valuable listed company.
What happened
Jio sold 270 million shares at 1,119 rupees each, raising 302 billion rupees ($3.2 billion) in the country's biggest IPO on record. CNBC and TechCrunch agree on the debut surge of more than 21%. On listing, Jio ranked behind only its parent, Reliance Industries, in market value among Indian listed companies.
Why it matters
A debut this large is a market verdict on India's consumer internet story, not just one company's stock. Meta and Google backing adds a second signal: global platforms are willing to price Indian digital growth as a core bet, not a side bet. For Indian markets, the listing also resets the scale of what a domestic IPO can absorb.
