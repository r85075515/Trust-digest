Why it matters
A personal scandal at the top of Nvidia has become a supply-chain and export-control risk overnight. If Washington tightens remote access to advanced chips, Chinese AI firms that depend on rented computing would feel it immediately — which is why they are moving now. The black-market price spike shows how fast scarcity, fear and speculation travel through the grey market.
