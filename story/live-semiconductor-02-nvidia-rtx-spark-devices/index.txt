What happened
Tom's Hardware reports that RTX Spark devices are officially on sale, with the Surface Laptop Ultra and other RTX Spark laptops available starting at $2,599, and full model-by-model pricing and availability now revealed. The Register adds independent testing: its benchmarked RTX Spark notebook delivers serious local AI compute — a potent CPU, blisteringly fast integrated graphics, and up to 128GB of unified memory in a lightweight, ultra-premium package ready to run demanding models locally. But the price stings: systems start around $2,600 and run well north of $6,000 for top-spec trims.
Why it matters
RTX Spark is Nvidia's bid to own the "local AI workstation" category the way it owns the data center: if demanding models can run on a laptop with 128GB of unified memory, a whole class of developers stops renting cloud GPUs for prototyping. The Register's verdict — serious compute, serious price — frames the real question: whether the premium over a cloud bill pays off depends entirely on how many local inference hours you actually burn.
