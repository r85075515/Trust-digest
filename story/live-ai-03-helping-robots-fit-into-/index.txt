10:T713,Robust AI is building robots designed to fit into the human workplace rather than replace the people in it. Despite fears about robots replacing humans at work, there are still plenty of things humans can do far better than machines, and the startup builds robots designed to complement what humans do best, according to MIT News.

What happened
Robust AI, a startup, builds robots meant to complement human strengths instead of replacing workers. Its mobile robot, named Carter, resembles a utility cart on wheels and is designed to fit seamlessly alongside humans in fast-changing warehouse and factory environments. The short report frames the company around that idea of partnership: machines take the roles they suit, while people keep the work they do best.

Why it matters
Warehouses and factories change quickly, and a robot that can work beside people in that setting is more useful than one that needs humans cleared out of the way. Designing for complement rather than replacement also speaks to the fear that automation means job loss, offering a model in which robots extend what human workers can do. The cart-like shape of Carter underlines the point: it is built to share space, not to take it over.
