10:T685,Presidents Donald Trump and Xi Jinping held their third meeting of the year in Shenzhen on the afternoon of November 19, with Xi calling for stronger communication on trade and Trump saying the two sides agree on regulating artificial intelligence.
What happened
Xi told the meeting that China and the United States should strengthen communication in trade and economic affairs. Trump later said in a Fox News interview that he and Xi both want AI regulated for safety reasons and will work together to draw up reasonable rules for managing the technology. The meeting was the third between the two leaders this year.
Why it matters
Three leader-level meetings in a single year, capped by talk of jointly setting AI safety rules, show both sides are at least willing to negotiate on technology governance. Whether the stated consensus becomes concrete rules will shape compliance costs and export-control expectations for the global AI industry.
Sources agree / disagree
Both CNA reports, one from its cross-strait desk and one from its international desk, agree on the meeting's timing and place and on both leaders' statements about AI regulation. The two items come from the same outlet, however, so no independent news organisation has corroborated the account yet.
Still uncertain
What the proposed AI safety rules would actually cover, and whether any timetable exists, is not stated in the reports, and it is unclear whether the trade discussion produced concrete outcomes. Official readouts from both sides will be needed for the details.
