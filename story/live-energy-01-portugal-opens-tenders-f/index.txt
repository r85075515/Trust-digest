10:T656,Portugal has opened two competitive procedures to allocate up to 1,050 MVA of grid connection capacity to energy storage projects, with 750 MVA reserved for standalone installations and 300 MVA for renewable power plants with co-located storage. Applications can be submitted until December 22, 2026. The government announced the two procedures in June, initially scheduling their launch for September 14.
What happened
Portugal launched the two tender procedures for grid connection capacity for storage. Standalone battery installations can bid for 750 MVA, while renewable plants that add storage on site can bid for 300 MVA. The application window runs to December 22, 2026, after a delay from the original September 14 start.
Why it matters
Storage is the bottleneck for renewable energy: solar and wind are cheap to build but useless when the grid cannot take their power. A 1,050 MVA tender is Portugal putting serious capacity behind that bottleneck. The takeaway is that the country is moving from announcing storage ambitions to actually allocating grid space for it.
Sources agree / disagree
The tender itself is reported by PV Magazine Global alone; the second item in the cluster, a Utility Dive sponsored piece, covers a different topic — a study finding $98 million in missed revenue across 30 batteries, and better software controls. Trust label: Single source — the tender rests on PV Magazine's report alone.
Still uncertain
