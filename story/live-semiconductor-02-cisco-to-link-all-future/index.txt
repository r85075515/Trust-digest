10:T6bc,Cisco will no longer create products that do not integrate with Cloud Control, its SaaS-y management platform that offers network admins a single tool to manage all of the networking giant's devices and much of its software portfolio. The networking giant announced Cloud Control in June 2025 and has only made it available to customers in the USA so far.
What happened
Cisco announced that every future product will be tied to Cloud Control, its cloud management platform. The rollout has been slow since the June 2025 announcement, and the platform is still limited to U.S. customers. Separately, Anthropic has started extending access to its bug-finding Mythos model, allowing Cisco and IBM to build it into their security products; Cisco announced the integration at an event in Melbourne, Australia, where it said the red-teaming tools in its AI Defense suite will probe AI models, agents, and applications for weaknesses.
Why it matters
Tying the whole product line to one cloud platform is Cisco's subscription-era bet: one management pane for everything it sells. The Mythos integration is the other half of the story — it puts an AI model that finds software bugs into enterprise security products. The takeaway is that Cisco is selling control: one platform to manage the network, and AI tools to test it.
Sources agree / disagree
Both items come from a single outlet, The Register, so there is no independent corroboration. Trust label: Single source — one outlet, two items, no cross-check in the snippets.
Still uncertain
