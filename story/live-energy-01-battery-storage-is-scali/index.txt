Advancing technologies and market dynamics keep driving divergent prices, and in the Middle East and Africa, utility-scale solar already leads.
What happened
Wood Mackenzie's LCOE report compared four-hour battery storage against open-cycle gas turbines across 43 markets and found storage cheaper to install in every one of them. The finding lands as battery storage keeps scaling fast and reshaping power systems toward a more flexible grid. The other item in this cluster, a Utility Dive piece on how utilities can trust storage to perform, is labeled sponsored content.
Why it matters
The judgment is economic, not environmental: the peaker-plant business case just broke. When storage undercuts gas turbines on cost everywhere modeled, utilities have a straight financial reason to reprice capacity plans around batteries. Expect procurement pipelines, not just pilot projects, to move.
