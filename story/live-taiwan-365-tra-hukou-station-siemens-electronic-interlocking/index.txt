10:T750,What happened
Taiwan Railway Corporation's Hukou Station obtained safety operational certification from TÜV Rheinland on November 27, 2026, officially commissioning its upgraded electronic interlocking system and integrating operations into Centralized Traffic Control (CTC). Hukou is the first of 68 stations across roughly 450 kilometers of track covered by Taiwan Railway's interlocking modernization program. The project deploys Siemens Mobility's Trackguard Westrace Mk II electronic interlocking platform alongside a 10-year maintenance commitment from Siemens.

Why it matters
Replacing legacy relay-based signaling with computer-based electronic interlocking and centralized control improves train routing reliability, fault diagnostics, and operational safety across Taiwan's heavily trafficked rail corridor.

Source differences
Siemens' official press release "Siemens to complete first Milestone in modernizing Taiwan's railway system" highlights the Trackguard Westrace Mk II architecture, the 68-station and 450-kilometer scope, and the 10-year maintenance agreement. QNC News' report "Hukou Becomes First of 68 Taiwan Stations to Move to Electronic Interlocking Under Siemens Upgrade" summarizes the Hukou cutover within the 68-station modernization rollout. Sunmedia's article "臺鐵『號誌聯鎖系統更新案』首站湖口站取得安全營運證明納入中央控制系統使用" specifies the November 27, 2026 commissioning date, the TÜV Rheinland safety certification, and Hukou Station's integration into centralized traffic control.

Still uncertain
The target completion schedule and rollout sequence for the remaining 67 stations across the 450-kilometer rail network have not been detailed in the cited releases.
