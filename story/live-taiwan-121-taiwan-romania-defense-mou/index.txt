Association Chairman Ming-Wei Wu (吳明蔚) signed a memorandum of understanding (MOU) with the Chief Executive Officer of the Romanian Defense Industry Employers' Organization (OPIA) to formalize bilateral industrial exchanges. Participating Taiwanese and Romanian companies held multiple business meetings during the forum.

Why it matters
