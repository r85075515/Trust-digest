10:T663,What happened
Belgium inaugurated Europe's largest satellite manufacturing facility in the southern city of Charleroi on November 24, 2026, in the presence of King Philippe. Operated by Walloon satellite maker Aerospacelab, the plant is designed for serial spacecraft production with an annual capacity of up to 500 satellites.

Why it matters
European governments and defense planners have sought independent low-Earth-orbit infrastructure amid heavy reliance on U.S. constellations such as Starlink and Starshield. Scaling serial assembly in Charleroi provides domestic manufacturing throughput for Earth observation and communications missions.

Source differences
ZME Science ("Europe Opens Massive Satellite Factory to Challenge Elon Musk's Starlink and Starshield") frames the factory as a strategic effort to reduce European dependence on Elon Musk's space networks. Militarnyi ("Belgium Opens Europe’s Largest Satellite Factory, With Annual Capacity of 500 Spacecraft") emphasizes serial production specifications and the 500-spacecraft annual output figure. EUalive ("King Philippe inaugurates Europe’s largest satellite factory near Charleroi") highlights King Philippe's attendance at the Marcinelle site near Charleroi and reports an investment of roughly 100 million euros.

Still uncertain
Actual first-year production volume, customer contract allocations between commercial and defense buyers, and delivery timelines for initial satellite batches remain undisclosed.
