Lake Havasu City Police arrested 29-year-old Damian Trumble on Thanksgiving evening, Nov. 26, after his 18-month-old child fell into a backyard swimming pool near Rainbow and Southwest boulevards and later died at Havasu Regional Medical Center. Authorities stated that Trumble had fallen asleep in a backyard playhouse prior to the incident and faces negligent homicide and child abuse charges.

「Why it matters」
Backyard swimming pool drownings remain a leading cause of accidental death among toddlers in Arizona, particularly during holiday gatherings when adult supervision routines can lapse. The criminal charges reflect local law enforcement's strict accountability standards regarding caregiver supervision around unfenced or accessible residential pools.
