10:T73f,What happened
The finals of the 21st Golden Shield Cybersecurity Competition took place on November 27, 2026, at the Taipei Silicon Valley International Conference Center. Out of 224 registered teams, 40 advanced to the Capture the Flag (CTF) finals testing network backdoor forensics, packet analysis, and endpoint defense. National Yang Ming Chiao Tung University's team "BambooB33F" won the university division, while a joint team from Affiliated Senior High School of NYCU, National Fengshan Senior High School, and Taoyuan Municipal Yang Ming Senior High School won the high school division. Coupang and DEVCORE participated as inaugural corporate prize sponsors.

Why it matters
Director-General Fu-Lung Tsai (蔡福隆) of the Administration for Cyber Security under the Ministry of Digital Affairs noted that domestic and international organizations face growing impacts from AI agent-driven cyberattacks. He stated that rapid artificial intelligence deployment will elevate the strategic value of cybersecurity, potentially turning it into Taiwan's next "sacred mountain" industry alongside semiconductors.

Source comparison
CNA, Economic Daily News (UDN), and KNews all covered the November 27 competition finals and Tsai's remarks. CNA focused on the CTF competition format and winning student teams, while Economic Daily News and KNews highlighted Coupang's first-time corporate sponsorship and Taiwan-Japan CISO Hao-Wei Chen's (陳浩維) past background as an early Golden Shield competitor.

What remains uncertain
Officials did not announce specific government budget allocations, tax incentives, or revenue growth targets to quantify how the cybersecurity sector will scale into a pillar industry.
