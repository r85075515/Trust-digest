10:T7a4,What happened
On November 27, 2026, Liquid AI released d1.3-ultra, the latest iteration in its specialized d1 family built for structured decision-making workflows. Built on top of d1-ultra using a new recursive improvement methodology just 12 days after its prior update, the model achieved a score of 76.4 on the Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1—tying Bison 31B for the top ranking while keeping API token pricing unchanged.

Why it matters
Enterprise deployment of autonomous decision agents is often constrained by inference costs at scale. By matching frontier decision accuracy at an estimated 133 USD per million tasks compared with Bison 31B's 345 USD—roughly a 60 percent cost reduction—d1.3-ultra lowers unit economics for high-volume routing, triage, and automated workflow selection.

Source differences
Crypto Briefing ("Liquid AI's d1.3-ultra ties Bison 31B for top decision index score at a") reports the November 27 launch date, the 76.4 score on Artificial Analysis Decision Index v2.1, and the approximate 60 percent cost advantage per million tasks. i10X News ("Liquid AI d1.3-ultra Ties Bison 31B on Decision Index at 61% Lower Task Cost") specifies the exact per-million-task cost comparison of 133 USD versus 345 USD for Bison 31B, while BlockBeats ("Liquid AI时隔12天再升级决策模型，借助递归改进重夺榜首- BlockBeats") notes the 12-day iteration cadence since d1-ultra and unchanged API input token pricing.

Still uncertain
Independent third-party production latency benchmarks, full model parameter counts, and whether the recursive refinement pipeline introduces extra inference overhead under complex multi-step enterprise workloads have not yet been audited outside initial benchmark disclosures.
