10:T6f2,What happened
Taiwan's Ministry of Education hosted the 2026 Humanities, Social Sciences and Industry Practice Innovation Link (iLink) Academic Symposium and Outcomes Showcase at Shih Chien University on November 27, 2026. The event brought together 33 universities and colleges, over 100 faculty members, and industry representatives, featuring 35 research papers and 15 outcome posters. It showcased results from the 2023–2026 (academic years 112–115) iLink program on cross-disciplinary innovation, industry-academia co-creation, and curriculum transformation in the AI era, reviewed sub-programs such as the AI Faculty Academy and the AI Teaching Application Competency Certification (AIEA, with over 2,100 participants), and outlined future plans for "iLink+".

Why it matters
As generative AI reshapes workplace skill demands, humanities and social science departments face pressure to integrate digital tools with real-world industry problems. The showcase demonstrates institutional efforts across 33 Taiwanese universities to standardize faculty AI literacy and connect liberal arts curricula with industry practice.

Source differences
Both articles stem from the Ministry of Education's event release. Youth Daily News titled its report "教育部整合產學研界展現人社跨域新能量", while Mega News titled its coverage "教育部iLink計畫成果發表 33校35篇論文展現人社跨域創新", both relaying the same participation counts and program milestones.

Still uncertain
Specific budget allocations, implementation timelines, and participating university quotas for the upcoming "iLink+" phase have not yet been detailed in the reports.
