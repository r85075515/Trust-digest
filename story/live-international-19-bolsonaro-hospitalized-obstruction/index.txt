10:T8f5,Jair Bolsonaro, Brazil's former president, was hospitalized in Brasília on Friday after severe abdominal pain and distension; tests showed a partial but near-complete intestinal obstruction, his doctor Brasil Caiado told journalists, and a scan showed damage to his small intestine and signs of inflammation. The 71-year-old is clinically stable, medicated, conscious and calm, the doctor said, and will remain under observation for 24 hours while doctors attempt to reverse the obstruction without an invasive procedure.

What happened: Bolsonaro, who governed from 2019 to 2022, is serving a 27-year prison sentence for plotting a coup after losing the 2022 election to President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva, and has been under humanitarian house arrest on medical grounds. His son Carlos, who was permitted to stay with him, described an "extremely severe abdominal distension" on arrival and said his father's health has been deteriorating. Bolsonaro has been hospitalized repeatedly and undergone more than nine surgeries since he was stabbed in the abdomen during a 2018 campaign event.

Why it matters: This is the latest turn in the long medical-political saga of Brazil's most polarizing figure: his health is the legal basis for his house arrest rather than a prison cell, so every hospitalization feeds the national argument over whether he should remain at home, return to custody, or receive further medical relief. A near-complete obstruction in a 71-year-old with his surgical history is a genuinely dangerous condition even when the first bulletin says stable.

Sources agree / disagree: Reuters and CNN agree on the diagnosis (near-complete obstruction), the treating doctor's account, the stable condition and the 24-hour observation plan. The political framing differs by source: his son attributes the deterioration to his confinement conditions; the medical team's public account is strictly clinical.

Still uncertain: Whether the obstruction resolves without surgery; whether he will need another operation — which would be at least his tenth since 2018; and whether this episode changes his custody arrangements.
