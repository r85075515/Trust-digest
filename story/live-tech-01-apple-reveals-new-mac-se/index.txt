What happened
Following an October announcement, Apple detailed new permission controls, warnings, and transparency features coming to macOS 27.3. After sounding an alarm about AI agents' access to Mac disks, Apple is taking steps to limit that disk access, TechCrunch and Engadget report.
Why it matters
An AI agent that can read a whole disk is a new kind of privacy hole, and operating-system permission gates put the user back in control. Apple's move sets the terms for how much an agent may see before it acts, which matters to every Mac user running one.
