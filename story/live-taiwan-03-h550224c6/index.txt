What happened
CNA correspondent Zhang Qian reported from Hong Kong on November 27, citing reports that students at a Sichuan school were photographed by a teacher gathering evidence and then punished for speaking the Sichuan dialect during break. The case drew public attention. Local officials later said they had ordered the school to abolish the improper rule immediately and had publicly criticized the teacher involved.

Why it matters
Break time is not class time, and punishing students for speaking a dialect polices language, not order. The swift official order to scrap the rule, paired with public criticism of the teacher, amounts to an admission that the rule could not stand — and that a school cannot draw the line on dialect use by itself.
