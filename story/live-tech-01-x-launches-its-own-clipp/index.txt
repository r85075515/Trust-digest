10:T5dd,X is getting into clipping. The platform has launched its own feature for cutting long videos into short, shareable clips — the format that fuels viral reach and influencer growth — in a bid to keep creators and their audiences on X.

What happened
TechCrunch reports that X has launched its own clipping feature. Clipping is the popular practice of cutting long videos into short clips that can go viral, and X's version is aimed at spurring influencer engagement: giving creators a native way to make the short-form content that builds audiences.

Why it matters
Short clips are the growth engine of social video, and the creators who make them are the audience. If creators can clip natively on X, they have less reason to do that work on rival platforms and bring the audience back later. For X, the feature is a direct play for creator activity that currently flows to TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

Sources agree / disagree
This is a single-outlet report from TechCrunch, so no second source confirms the launch details or the feature's scope. The feed summary gives no rollout dates, eligibility or clip length limits, so there is nothing to cross-check.

Still uncertain
Which accounts get access first, how long clips can be, and whether clipping ties into creator monetization are all unclear from the summary. The full TechCrunch piece is needed for those specifics.
