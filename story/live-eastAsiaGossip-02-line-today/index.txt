10:T661,Thirty years of Taiwanese television history just closed a chapter.
What happened
Multiple Taiwanese outlets report that the long-running entertainment program Showbiz (完全娛樂) is ending its television edition. UDN, via LINE TODAY, reports exclusively that the production team confirmed the TV version is 'retiring with honor' and will focus on the online edition going forward. Host Lulu confirmed the next step herself: UDN Showbiz, Yahoo News, Liberty Times Entertainment, and Next Apple News all carry her confirmation, her account of what comes next, and her thanks to SETN for its support. The coverage notes the show's 30th anniversary as the backdrop for the change.
Why it matters
A 30-year TV run ending is a milestone in any market, and the destination — online-only — says everything about where Taiwanese entertainment audiences have gone. For the production team, the shift trades broadcast reach for digital flexibility; for viewers, it is another familiar TV habit migrating to phones. Lulu's personal confirmation, rather than a corporate statement, also shows how host-driven these transitions have become.
Sources agree / disagree
UDN／LINE TODAY, UDN Showbiz, Yahoo News, Liberty Times Entertainment, and Next Apple News all agree: the TV edition is ending and the online edition is the future, with Lulu personally confirming. No outlet disputes the move. Trust label: Multi-source agreement — five outlets align on the transition.
