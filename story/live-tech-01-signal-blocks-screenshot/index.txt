10:T6d6,Signal has switched on screenshot blocking by default on iOS 27 and Android, aiming to stop AI agents from reading private chats, Engadget and The Decoder report. The company calls the practice an 'existential threat' to privacy, but both outlets agree the protection is still incomplete.

What happened
Signal now blocks screenshots by default on iOS 27 and Android so AI agents such as Apple's Siri, Google's Gemini and Meta's Muse cannot capture private conversations. The Decoder reports the move as a direct response to operating-system features that let AI assistants read on-screen content. Signal admits gaps remain, including on macOS, and the coverage calls for a simple way for developers to opt out of AI snooping. The article argues Apple, Google and Microsoft should follow suit with similar protections.

Why it matters
Private chats are only private if the operating system cannot hand them to an AI, and Signal is treating that as a design problem rather than a settings tweak. If the biggest platforms copy the block, screenshot-based snooping gets harder across the board; if they do not, Signal users on other devices stay exposed.

Sources agree / disagree
Engadget and The Decoder report the same core facts: default screenshot blocking on iOS 27 and Android, the AI-agent rationale, and the admission that the fix is incomplete. No numeric or factual conflicts appeared in the feed snippets.

Still uncertain
The snippets do not say when the macOS gap will close, how the block behaves with accessibility tools, or whether Apple, Google or Microsoft have responded. Check the publishers' full reports for those details.
