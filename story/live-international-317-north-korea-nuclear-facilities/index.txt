According to North Korea's state-run Korean Central News Agency (KCNA) on November 26, 2026, leader Kim Jong Un (Kim Jong Un) toured facilities producing weapons-grade nuclear material and conducting nuclear weapons research at an undisclosed location. State media quoted Kim as calling North Korea's nuclear-armed status an "absolutely irreversible choice" and ordering the military to expand the deployment of tactical nuclear weapons across multiple operational domains.

Why it matters
The inspection and state media imagery—which outside analysts noted appeared to show uranium enrichment centrifuges and a suspected nuclear-powered submarine reactor—reinforce Pyongyang's rejection of denuclearization talks following recent diplomatic overtures by U.S. President Donald Trump (Donald Trump).
