France's Hellfest Open Air Festival unveiled the complete lineup for its 20th anniversary edition, set for June 17–20, 2027, in Clisson, France. The milestone festival will feature 308 bands representing 28 countries across 10 stages—four of them newly added. Mainstage 1 will be headlined by Blink-182 (blink-182) on Thursday, Parkway Drive on Friday, Faith No More on Saturday, and French metal act Gojira on Sunday in what will be Gojira's only European performance of 2027. Mainstage 2 headliners include Mötley Crüe, Marilyn Manson, Judas Priest, and Evanescence. Organizers also introduced the "Extreme Zone," a four-stage sub-festival showcasing 124 death, thrash, heavy, and black metal bands, alongside 163 first-time Hellfest performers.

為什麼重要
Expanding to 10 stages and 308 acts—while securing exclusive European festival appearances from Gojira and rare reunions such as Faith No More and Children of Bodom—cements Hellfest's status as one of Europe's largest heavy music gatherings.

來源異同
Consequence, MetalTalk, New Noise Magazine, and Sonic Perspectives all confirm the June 17–20, 2027 dates, the 308-band count across 10 stages, the mainstage headliners, Gojira's exclusive European date, and the 124-band Extreme Zone.

仍不確定
Single-day ticket release dates, stage-by-stage set times, and whether any late-stage lineup replacements will occur ahead of June 2027 remain to be announced.
