What happened
According to NPR Education, Google is preparing to cut off Search access for schools that have not adopted AI. The move has not yet taken effect, and the report frames it as forthcoming. Parents and educators quoted in the report are frustrated and are asking what Google's motivations are.

Why it matters
Search is a basic tool for classrooms, and losing it would change how teachers and students find information. Tying access to AI adoption would also put pressure on schools that have chosen, for cost, privacy or policy reasons, to keep AI out of the classroom.
