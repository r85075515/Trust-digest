10:T6c4,Elon Musk said ousting Jensen Huang would be like 'kicking Michael Jordan off your team,' defending the Nvidia chief executive during an appearance on Wednesday at The New York Times' DealBook Summit.
What happened
Musk said he did not think Huang was disloyal to the United States, and that he was not sure what the term 'MAGA' meant. He said he was 'not MAGA,' distancing himself from the political movement linked to Trump, and called his record spending on the US midterms 'pretty foolish.' Musk also said Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy was lying about disruptions to Starshield in Ukraine and trying to misuse SpaceX's consumer service, Starlink, for military purposes, as he tried to explain the conflict after weeks of back and forth between Zelenskyy, Musk and the US government.
Why it matters
The remarks do two things at once: they put Musk publicly on Huang's side as the Nvidia spy scandal hangs over the Trump–Xi meeting, and they put distance between Musk and the MAGA movement he was once closely tied to. The Starlink claims add a third front, with Musk accusing a wartime ally's president of lying about a US company's service.
Sources agree / disagree
BBC Business, TechCrunch and The Verge cover different remarks from the same DealBook appearance — the midterm spending, the 'I'm not MAGA' line, the Huang defence and the Ukraine–Starlink explanation — and they agree on the occasion and the quotes. No numeric or name conflicts appear in the snippets.
Still uncertain
