What happened
Microsoft says a bad update — not a cyberattack or AI — caused its servers around the world to crash. The outage lasted about 24 hours before service began returning, according to Wired.

Why it matters
Microsoft 365 is a daily workplace platform, so a full day offline disrupts email, documents and meetings for its users. The cause matters too: a faulty update points to internal change management rather than an external attacker, which changes what customers should expect from the fix. The outage also shows how a single faulty update can spread across a global service before it is caught, and why the recovery was gradual rather than instant.
