10:T7a8,What happened
On November 27, 2026, Wieson Technologies (stock code 6272) announced a partnership agreement with Liturex to establish the "W&L OptoLink Lab" (驊陞－光為高速光電互連電磁仿真聯合實驗室), led by Liturex founder Dr. Yunpeng Song (宋雲鵬博士) as chief technical expert. Combining Liturex's optical module design capabilities with Wieson's electromagnetic simulation and high-speed electrical interface expertise, the lab initially focuses on 800G and 1.6T interconnects for AI servers and data centers. According to the announcement, co-developed 1.6T optical modules compliant with OCTC specifications have entered mass production and delivery, near-packaged optics (NPO) system-level validation is underway, 3.2T modules have been sampled, and 6.4T and co-packaged optics (CPO) architectures are under evaluation.

Why it matters
As AI cluster bandwidth scales from 800G toward 1.6T and 3.2T, signal integrity and electromagnetic interference become primary bottlenecks; pairing optical engine design with precision electrical interface simulation accelerates high-speed transceiver commercialization.

Source differences
BusinessNews (PR Newswire) published "驊陞科技（6272）與光為科技簽訂成立電磁仿真聯合實驗室共建合作協定", while Wieson Technologies' official website published "驊陞科技攜手光為科技成立「電磁模擬聯合實驗室」，推進 1.6T 至 6.4T AI 光電互連佈局". Both items originate from Wieson's November 27 corporate press release and report identical product roadmap milestones.

Still uncertain
The announcement does not disclose financial investment figures for the joint lab, specific hyperscale customer names, or commercial mass-production timelines for the 3.2T and 6.4T product generations.
