10:T6ee,Apple finally has touchscreen laptops. At an event in Los Angeles, the company detailed new MacBook Pros with OLED XDR displays that respond to finger control, a light-up Apple logo, and its M5 Pro and M5 Max chips, and opened them for pre-order.
What happened
The 14-inch and 16-inch MacBook Pros are available for pre-order now and will ship on November 6. The 14-inch model starts at $2,899 and the 16-inch at $3,399. A fully upgraded configuration costs around $14,549. The new models use Apple's existing M5 Pro and M5 Max chips. Alongside the MacBook Pro, pre-orders also opened for a new OLED iPad mini, an M6 MacBook Pro, and a new M6 iMac, according to one outlet's roundup.
Why it matters
This is Apple's first touchscreen laptop, and the pricing is the story as much as the screen: the top configuration pushes a mainstream laptop line into territory usually reserved for workstation machines. Whether buyers accept that premium for touch and the PROLED display will test how far Apple's laptop pricing can stretch.
Sources agree / disagree
TechCrunch, Tom's Hardware, and 9to5Toys agree on the launch, the pre-order opening, and the starting prices. The apparent numeric disagreement in the feed data is not a real dispute: $14,549 is the fully upgraded price, while $2,899 and $3,399 are starting prices for the two sizes, so the figures describe different configurations.
