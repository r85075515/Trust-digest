10:T62f,Apple's first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, has run into screen trouble days after launch: the company has acknowledged that the inner display on a small number of units is faulty and will replace affected phones, CNA reported on October 26.
What happened
CNA, citing its San Francisco dispatch on October 26, reported that Apple acknowledged problems with the inner screen on some iPhone Duo units shortly after the first foldable iPhone went on sale, and will give affected users a replacement handset. The report does not give a number for how many units are affected, nor a cause for the faults.
Why it matters
The verdict is practical: for a first-generation foldable, the screen is the product. A fast, no-argument replacement offer limits the damage from early hardware faults, but every additional report will test whether this is a small batch issue or a durability question for the whole category. Buyers weighing a foldable purchase will watch how Apple handles the next few weeks.
Sources agree / disagree
Only CNA's technology feed covers this card's version of the story. Trust label: Single source — one outlet, no second source in the feed material. Note that the same feed batch also carried a separate Apple item, the new OLED iPad mini announcement, which is a different product story and is not expanded here.
Still uncertain
