10:T6ca,What happened
On November 27, 2026, beauty group Puig announced an agreement to acquire a 75 percent stake in Artessence from private equity firm Advent International for approximately 1.2 billion EUR, reflecting an implied total valuation of around 1.9 billion USD. Artessence holds fragrance brands Parfums de Marly and Initio Parfums Privés, while founder Julien Sprecher (朱利安・施普雷歇) will retain a 25 percent stake; the transaction is not yet completed and is expected to close in the second quarter of 2027.

Why it matters
Adding high-growth niche fragrance houses Parfums de Marly and Initio Parfums Privés expands Puig's luxury scent portfolio while keeping founder involvement via a 25 percent retained stake. Because the deal is only at the agreement stage, final completion depends on statutory consultation and regulatory clearance.

Source differences
Reuters, titled "Puig to buy Parfums de Marly owner from Advent in €1.2 billion deal", and Business of Fashion, titled "Puig Agrees to Acquire Parfums de Marly and Initio Parfums Privés for €1.2 Billion", both report the agreement to buy a 75 percent stake in Artessence for about 1.2 billion EUR from Advent, the roughly 1.9 billion USD valuation, Julien Sprecher's 25 percent retained stake, French employee representative consultation, regulatory approvals, and targeted Q2 2027 closing.

Still uncertain
Completion remains subject to French employee representative consultation procedures and antitrust or regulatory approvals, with final closing timing targeted for the second quarter of 2027.
