What happened
Musk told Sorkin, speaking virtually at the DealBook Summit on Wednesday, that he spent $442 million backing Republican campaigns in the 2026 midterms, where the party suffered sweeping losses to Democrats. He also called Nvidia chief Jensen Huang irreplaceable and discussed overlaps between SpaceX and Tesla. The Financial Times, describing the sum as 'nearly $500 million', reported that Musk is backing away from MAGA after his financial support for Donald Trump's party failed to sway voters.
Why it matters
A $442 million outlay from the world's richest person makes his public regret a notable signal: the spending did not deliver the result he wanted, and he is saying so on the record. His praise of Huang also underlines the Nvidia chief's standing among fellow tech leaders.
Sources agree / disagree
CNBC, The Hill and the Financial Times agree on the core facts: Musk regrets the spending and called Huang irreplaceable. The sums differ only in presentation — CNBC and The Hill cite $442 million, while the Financial Times rounds it to 'nearly $500 million' — the same order of magnitude, not a contradiction.
Still uncertain
The snippets do not give Musk's full remarks on Huang, the SpaceX–Tesla overlaps, or what he plans to spend next. The CNBC snippet cuts off before Musk's stated goal is given. Verify on the publisher pages.
