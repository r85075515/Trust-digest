10:T855,What happened
The U.S. Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC) announced that Zenara Commerce LLC of Sheridan, Wyoming is recalling approximately 210,410 4-in-1 Plug-in Steadfast Haven carbon monoxide (CO) and natural gas detectors, models KH158 and GC03 (Recall No. 27-109). Sold online at truststeadfast.com between February and September 2026 for 48 to 70 USD, the units can fail to sound an alarm when hazardous carbon monoxide levels are present, following one reported failure without injury.

Why it matters
Carbon monoxide is an odorless, colorless gas that causes acute poisoning, loss of consciousness, or death, especially during winter heating months when home furnaces and gas appliances run continuously. Because consumers rely on plug-in detectors as a primary life-safety warning, CPSC instructs owners to keep using the current unit until a free replacement arrives, then mark "RECALLED" with initials, upload photo proof for registration, and dispose of the defective detector.

Source differences
The CPSC official release "Zenara Recalls 4-in-1 Plug in Steadfast Haven Carbon Monoxide (CO) and Natural Gas Detectors Due to Risk of Serious Injury or Death from Failure to Alert Consumers to Hazardous Levels of Gas and CO" sets out the exact recall count (about 210,410), model numbers (KH158 and GC03), recall number (27-109), retailer details, and replacement registration procedure. KTVU FOX 2's "200K carbon monoxide detectors recalled over risk of serious injury", FOX 13 Tampa Bay's "200K carbon monoxide detectors recalled over risk of serious injury", and WPXI's "Recall alert: Carbon Monoxide, natural gas detectors recalled - WPXI" accurately relay the CPSC notice while rounding the unit figure to "more than 210,000" in body text and "200K" in headlines.

Still uncertain
Whether independent laboratory testing identified a specific sensor or firmware defect behind the alarm failure, and how many units have already been registered for replacement.
