10:T8ad,What happened
Golden State Medical Supply, Inc. (GSMS) of Camarillo, California initiated a voluntary nationwide consumer-level recall of one lot of Ibuprofen 800mg Tablets (NDC 51407-841-05, Lot GS069303, expiration date 01/2030) after identifying potential aluminum particulate contamination measuring 0.01 to 0.10 millimeters localized on the tablet surfaces. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) posted the safety notice, instructing patients to stop taking tablets from the affected lot and return unused portions to their pharmacy.

Why it matters
Prescription-strength 800mg ibuprofen is widely prescribed for osteoarthritis, rheumatoid arthritis, and moderate pain, and continued ingestion of metallic particulates can cause minor irritation or abrasion of the stomach and intestinal linings. While regulators noted that the likelihood of a serious adverse event is remote given the microscopic particle size, targeted lot-level recalls prevent prolonged gastrointestinal exposure among chronic pain patients.

Source differences
The FDA's official recall notice "Golden State Medical Supply, Inc. Issues a Voluntary Nationwide Recall of Ibuprofen 800mg Tablets Due to Potential Aluminum Particulate Contamination Localized on the Surface of the Tablets | FDA" provides the exact NDC code (51407-841-05), lot number (GS069303), expiration date (01/2030), and particle dimensions (0.01 to 0.10 mm). Secondary news reports—The Hill's "Ibuprofen recalled nationwide over contaminent: FDA - The Hill", Daily Voice's "Golden State Medical Supply Recalls Ibuprofen, FDA Says", and WJXT News4JAX's "Company recalls 800mg ibuprofen tablets over aluminum particle contamination"—summarize the FDA notice accurately, with The Hill adding that the lot was distributed through medical wholesaler McKesson, which was instructed to quarantine remaining stock.

Still uncertain
The total number of bottles distributed in Lot GS069303 and the specific manufacturing equipment source of the surface aluminum particles were not disclosed in the recall announcement.
