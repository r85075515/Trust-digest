What happened
After a four-week trial, the court sentenced Brand to 10.5 years. The convictions cover a rape in 2009 and sexual assaults against three other women. The sentencing judge called Brand a 'sexual predator' when handing down the term. NPR, the BBC, CBS News, and The Guardian all report the same sentence.
Why it matters
This is the sentencing follow-up to the guilty verdicts reported on November 6, when the expected range was six to nine years — the actual term of ten and a half years is longer than that early estimate. The judge's 'sexual predator' characterization also matters: it frames how the court views the pattern of offending, not just the individual counts. The takeaway is that the case has now moved from verdict to punishment, and the sentence is at the severe end of expectations.
