What happened
The $33.2 billion figure is Azure's quarterly revenue in dollars, disclosed by Microsoft for the first time. In the same batch of TechCrunch reporting, Microsoft said it made $10 billion in revenue from commercial arrangements with OpenAI, including revenue-sharing payments, in the quarter ending September 30. That second figure is a different number about a different relationship, not a rival estimate of Azure's revenue.
Why it matters
A dollar figure for Azure turns a long-running guessing game into a measurable race: the cloud market's number two can now be compared directly with AWS. The $10 billion OpenAI figure matters in the other direction, showing that the partnership is not only a cost for Microsoft but a major revenue line tied to OpenAI's business.
