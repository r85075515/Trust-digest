10:T5d8,Taiwan stocks opened lower and stayed weak on Monday: the index closed down 289.18 points at 46,822.08, off 0.61%, on turnover of NT$825.1 billion, CNA reports.
What happened
Weak US tech stocks on Friday dragged overnight index futures, and Taiwan stocks opened lower and slid — plunging more than 940 points intraday to as low as 46,170, with electronics heavyweights and the OTC index both soft. Bargain hunting then emerged, narrowing the decline into the close: the weighted index finished at 46,822.08, down 289.18 points or 0.61%, on NT$825.1 billion in turnover. TSMC pared its losses into the close to finish flat at NT$2,420, per CNA's later item.
Why it matters
The session shows both Taiwan's linkage to US tech sentiment — Friday's weakness set the tone before the local open — and the market's dip-buying reflex: a 940-point intraday washout still found buyers. TSMC closing flat after a weak day underscores its anchor role.
Sources agree / disagree
CNA's items agree with each other on the 289.18-point drop, the 46,822.08 close and TSMC at 2,420 flat. But an Economic Daily headline in the same cluster reports something very different — a 1,655-point plunge to 46,969 with TSMC down 2% at 2,440 — which conflicts sharply with CNA's numbers and looks like a stale or mismatched feed item.
