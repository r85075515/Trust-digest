10:T534,Acer says its Taiwan consumer PC shipments rose 15% year on year in January-October, despite rising prices and shortages of key components. The company now expects the Double 11 sales period and the fourth-quarter peak season to lift growth even higher.

What happened
Acer told Central News Agency (CNA) on 3 November that Taiwan consumer PC shipments grew 15% year on year in the first ten months, even as component price rises and shortages weighed on supply. With the Double 11 promotion and the traditional fourth-quarter peak ahead, Acer expects shipment growth in November, December and the full year to exceed 15%.

Why it matters
Holding double-digit growth through a component squeeze shows demand is absorbing higher costs rather than collapsing. Whether the year ends above 15% now depends on holiday execution, not just underlying demand.

Sources agree / disagree
CNA and Economic Daily News carry the same 15% figure and the same above-15% full-year expectation, with no conflicting numbers in the available material. The two reports describe the same company statement.

Still uncertain
The material does not give absolute shipment volumes, the size of the component price rises, or which product lines drove growth. Those details would show how broad-based the 15% gain really is.
