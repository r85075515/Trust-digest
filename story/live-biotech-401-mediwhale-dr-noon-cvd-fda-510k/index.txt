10:T67a,What happened
South Korean medical AI company Mediwhale announced on November 27, 2026 that its retinal-AI cardiovascular risk assessment software, Dr. Noon CVD, received U.S. FDA 510(k) clearance. The software combines AI analysis of retinal images with patient age and sex to estimate an individual's 10-year risk of a first atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) event. It is indicated for adults aged 45 to 79 who have not previously been diagnosed with ASCVD, and has previously obtained regulatory authorization in the European Union and South Korea.

Why it matters
Dr. Noon CVD provides a non-invasive method to assess long-term cardiovascular risk using eye scans rather than traditional blood draws or cardiac imaging. Securing FDA 510(k) clearance opens access to the U.S. clinical market for primary and preventive care screening among middle-aged and older adults without prior ASCVD diagnosis.

Source differences
Both listed sources derive from Mediwhale's announcement. The PR Newswire press release titled "Mediwhale Announces First FDA 510(k) Clearance for Its Retinal-AI Cardiovascular Risk Assessment Software" serves as the company's official disclosure, while FirstWord HealthTech's report titled "FDA clears Mediwhale's eye scan tech to detect heart disease risk" summarizes the same regulatory milestone for healthcare industry readers.

Still uncertain
