10:T52f,India's Nifty index has rebounded almost 15% since early October, pushing valuations higher, but rising bond yields may now challenge that sharp recovery.
What happened
Bloomberg reports that the Nifty has climbed almost 15% from its early-October level, lifting valuations across the market. Rising bond yields may pose a challenge to the rebound, as higher yields raise borrowing costs and put pressure on the equity valuations built up during the rally.
Why it matters
After a 15% run, valuations are stretched, and rising yields make money more expensive, squeezing the room for stocks to keep climbing. Investors holding Indian equities should watch the bond market next, not just the index's gains.
Sources agree / disagree
Only Bloomberg has reported this story, so there is no second outlet to corroborate the rebound figure or the direction of yields.
Still uncertain
The report gives no yield figures, no index levels and no estimate of how much or how soon higher yields might weigh on the market. Whether bonds and equities move together will only become clear from forthcoming market data. The report also gives no guidance on whether the rally reflects earnings growth or positioning, and it does not say which sectors led the rebound.
