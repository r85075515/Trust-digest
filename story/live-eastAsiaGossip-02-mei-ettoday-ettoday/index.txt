10:Tadf,In an ETtoday exclusive, entertainer A-di revealed that one celebrity couple actually divorced after joking about divorce on a show — and admitted his own marriage to his wife Mei nearly broke down as well. On the same news cycle, Taiwanese Hokkien singer Chang Hsiu-ching dropped her own bombshell: she secretly divorced her second husband Wang Kuo-tsan three years ago.
What happened
ETtoday's exclusive has A-di disclosing that an entertainer couple's on-show divorce talk turned into a real split, while his own marriage with Mei also came close to collapse. Separately, 56-year-old Chang Hsiu-ching announced on the 16th that she had already divorced her second husband Wang Kuo-tsan — 12 years her junior — three years ago. She said she lent him NT$34 million out of goodwill, let him keep living in her home, and never received two years of concert pay, calling on him to face his responsibilities; he has gone silent. Her first husband, former police officer-turned-singer Lin Hung-ming, is now running for village chief in Kenting, and the two remain on friendly terms years after their split. One report described her as lamenting she was left NT$36 million in debt for her ex-husband — a figure that conflicts with the NT$34 million cited elsewhere. When asked about the matter, entertainer Tsai Chang-hsien — once called a "good brother" by Wang on Facebook — declined to comment through his agent.
Why it matters
Two marriage-crisis stories landing on the same day is peak Taiwanese entertainment news: one a near-miss told as a cautionary tale, the other a three-year secret detonated with money at its center. Chang's case has the sharper teeth — a beloved veteran singer framing herself as the victim of a younger ex-husband's debts turns a private split into a public morality play, and the NT$34M-versus-NT$36M discrepancy guarantees the story a second news cycle. A-di's revelation works differently: by naming the near-breakdown in his own marriage, he buys credibility for the gossip about others.
Sources agree / disagree
Liberty Times, ETtoday, Yahoo TW and SETN converge on Chang's secret three-year divorce, the NT$34 million loan, the missing concert pay, and Wang going silent. They diverge on the money: most cite NT$34 million lent, while one report frames it as NT$36 million in debt she carried for him — a genuine numeric conflict in the material. On A-di and Mei, ETtoday stands alone with the exclusive.
Still uncertain
The snippets do not include any response from Wang Kuo-tsan himself, the exact timeline of the divorce three years ago, or what "concert pay" was withheld and by whom.
