What happened
PBS NewsHour reported the announcement and its stakes for Americans and the economy, with Lisa Desjardins discussing the impact with Gavin Bade of The Wall Street Journal. Why it matters
The tariffs land on both of America's largest trading relationships at once, and the two responses point in opposite directions: Brussels is contesting the premise in public, while Beijing is holding its fire. That split decides whether the episode stays a negotiating lever or becomes a new round of retaliation.
