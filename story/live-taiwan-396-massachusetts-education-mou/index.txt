10:T63f,What happened
Taiwan's Ministry of Education and the Massachusetts State Department of Elementary and Secondary Education signed a K-12 education cooperation memorandum of understanding on Nov. 27, 2026. Department of International and Cross-strait Education Director-General Lee Yu-chuan and Massachusetts Commissioner Pedro Martinez signed the pact, witnessed by Deputy Minister of Education Liu Kuo-wei.

Why it matters
The agreement establishes structured channels for exchanging course materials, recruiting language teachers, and running mutual teaching assistant programs. During the visit, Martinez and his chief of staff Lauren Secatore also met local education officials in Kaohsiung, Tainan, Chiayi City, and Taichung to discuss bilingual and digital education.

Source differences
Taipei Times ("Massachusetts signs MOU with education ministry - Taipei Times") highlights that Massachusetts joins 28 other U.S. states in cooperation talks and notes the 2023 exchange of 21 Massachusetts high school students studying Mandarin in Taiwan. Still uncertain
Neither report specifies the exact annual quota of exchange teachers and students, the duration of the memorandum, or the dedicated budget allocated by either side for the upcoming academic year.
