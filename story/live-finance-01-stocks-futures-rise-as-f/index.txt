What happened

Why it matters
The judgment is market-mechanical: a Fed chair who won't validate hike bets after hot inflation data is telling traders the bar for October is high. The rally is the market repricing odds, not new easing — don't confuse relief with a pivot.
Sources agree / disagree

Still uncertain
