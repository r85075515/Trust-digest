What happened

Why it matters
The final Sunday is when campaigns spend their biggest names, and the Kuomintang played its two most recognizable figures, Han and Lu, the bald one and the swallow, in one motorcade. The takeaway is that both camps treated the twin cities as the decisive front: the KMT put its star power on the road, while the DPP answered with its own heavyweights rather than conceding the weekend.
