10:T67e,What happened
The U.S. Department of Justice announced through its Office of Community Oriented Policing Services (COPS Office) that it has awarded nearly 150 million USD under the 2026 COPS Hiring Program (CHP). The federal grants will fund the hiring of more than 1,000 law enforcement officers and deputies across state, local, and tribal police agencies.

Why it matters
Police departments across the United States have faced persistent recruitment and staffing shortages. The competitive federal grants directly subsidize entry-level officer salaries and benefits, helping local jurisdictions expand community policing and crime-reduction initiatives under Operation Take Back America and Project Safe Neighborhoods.

Source differences
The U.S. Department of Justice Office of Public Affairs release ("Department of Justice Announces Nearly $150 Million to Hire Over 1,000 Law Enforcement Officers and Deputies") details the nationwide 2026 COPS Hiring Program awards totaling nearly 150 million USD. A regional release from the U.S. Attorney's Office for the Southern District of Georgia ("Department of Justice Announces Nearly $150 Million to Hire Over 1,000 Law Enforcement Officers and Deputies") links the funding to Operation Take Back America and Project Safe Neighborhoods.

Still uncertain
The full state-by-state breakdown of individual agency awards and the timeline for recipient departments to complete academy recruitment and deployment were not included in the summary announcements.
