10:T687,Big pharma's obesity-drug land grab has a new headline deal — and it runs through China.
What happened
BioPharma Dive and ET Pharma both report that Roche has struck a $1.1 billion deal with China's Rona Therapeutics for an RNAi-based obesity drug aimed at lowering levels of 'visceral' fat. Under the agreement, Rona Therapeutics is set to receive an upfront payment of $100 million and is eligible for up to $1.06 billion in potential milestone payments, as well as tiered royalties on global net sales. BioPharma Dive notes the alliance extends a particularly active dealmaking stretch for Roche in China and makes the company the latest pharmaceutical giant to bet on medicines that target visceral fat.
Why it matters
The obesity-drug race is moving past first-generation treatments into targeted mechanisms like RNAi, and the deal sizes keep climbing. For Roche, buying into a China-originated RNAi asset signals that the next wave of weight-loss innovation may be licensed in from Chinese biotech rather than built in Basel — a reversal of the traditional flow. Milestone-heavy structures also show how both sides are pricing the risk of early-stage science.
Sources agree / disagree
BioPharma Dive and ET Pharma agree on the $1.1 billion headline, the $100 million upfront, and the up-to-$1.06 billion milestone structure; no conflicts appear in the snippets. Trust label: Two-source agreement — two outlets report the same deal terms.
Still uncertain
The development stage of the RNAi drug is not stated in the snippets, and milestone payments are potential, not guaranteed.
