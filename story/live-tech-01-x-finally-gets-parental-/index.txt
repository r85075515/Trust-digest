What happened
X, the social network owned by Elon Musk, has rolled out parental controls for teenage users, according to TechCrunch. The controls let parents and guardians manage what their children see on X. The move comes 20 years after the service launched. TechCrunch reports the addition; the feed summary gives no launch date, no list of settings, and no statement from X itself.

Why it matters
Parents have had no built-in way to manage what their teenagers see on X until now. The controls put that management inside X itself, giving families a direct tool on the platform for the first time.
