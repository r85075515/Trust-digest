10:T885,What happened
On November 27, 2026, Taiwan's Industrial Technology Research Institute (ITRI) and the Keio University Global Research Institute (KGRI) in Japan announced a strategic cooperation agreement to establish a cross-border digital trust network. The collaboration builds upon a three-tier architectural model proposed by KGRI and continues bilateral digital trust exchanges dating back to the 1999–2000 Asia Public Key Infrastructure (PKI) Forum. On the Taiwanese side, TWCA (Taiwan-CA Inc.) serves as the trust service provider, while Chunghwa Telecom supplies business certificates and a trust testing environment, as highlighted by ITRI Information and Communications Research Laboratories Deputy General Director Huang Wei-chung (Huang Wei-chung).

Why it matters
Cross-border authentication and verifiable credentials are essential foundations for secure digital trade, supply chain verification, and artificial intelligence governance. By operationalizing the Data Free Flow with Trust (DFFT) vision between Taiwan and Japan, the initiative creates an interoperable verification framework designed for future expansion into ASEAN, the European Union, India, and the United States.

Still uncertain
