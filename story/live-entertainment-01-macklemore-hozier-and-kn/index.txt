What happened
Macklemore kicked off his Free Palestine benefit concert series in Dublin on Monday night, October 26, bringing out Hozier, Kneecap and a stacked bill of guests at a sold-out 3Arena. Joining the Seattle rapper onstage were Irish folk band Beoga, singer-songwriter Lina Makoul, M1 of Dead Prez and DJ Habibeats, with Game of Thrones actor Liam Cunningham also on the bill. The show kicked off the rapper's series of charity concerts after he was removed from Ed Sheeran's tour for Free Palestine statements — and it featured Sheeran's own backing band. Proceeds from the tour, which begins in Dublin, will go to organisations supporting Palestinians, he says.
Why it matters
The verdict is about conversion: a career setback — losing a tour slot over political statements — has been turned into a headlining charity series with a sold-out arena and high-profile guests. For readers tracking artist activism, the Dublin night shows that political positioning can mobilize an audience and a lineup, not just cost one. The fundraising outcome, rather than the symbolism, is what will judge the tour.
