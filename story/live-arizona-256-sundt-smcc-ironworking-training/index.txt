10:T6ee,「What happened」
Sundt Construction and South Mountain Community College (SMCC) opened a new outdoor ironworking practice and training area in Phoenix, Arizona, serving both college students and current Sundt apprentices. Following institutional announcements by SMCC on November 19 and Sundt on November 24, construction trade outlets reported the facility's opening on November 26 and November 27, 2026, noting that Sundt employee-owners contributed to building the outdoor training space.

「Why it matters」
Large-scale commercial, industrial, and infrastructure projects across the Phoenix metro area have intensified competition for certified structural steel and ironworking tradespeople. Embedding a dedicated outdoor practice yard directly within a community college workforce program shortens the transition from classroom instruction to paid registered apprenticeships.

「Source differences」
Construction Owners published "Sundt Opens Ironworking Training Facility in Phoenix", highlighting that Sundt employee-owners contributed to constructing the outdoor training area and framing the project as an expansion of Arizona construction apprenticeship opportunities. Trade Vulcan published "Phoenix opened a new ironworking practice yard. Here's how to get", focusing on practical access pathways for students and existing Sundt apprentices looking to train on structural steel.

「Still uncertain」
Total annual trainee capacity, specific curriculum start dates for the upcoming academic semester, and the capital construction cost of the outdoor yard have not been publicly specified.
