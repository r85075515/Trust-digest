10:T637,What happened
The City of Phoenix Parks and Recreation Department announced that Phoenix Children's, Arizona's leading pediatric health system, will serve as grand marshal of the 2026 APS Electric Light Parade. The 39th annual event is scheduled to return to central Phoenix on Saturday, December 5, 2026, running from 5:00 PM to 11:00 PM and starting at Central Avenue and Montebello Avenue.

Why it matters
Honoring Phoenix Children's places pediatric healthcare workers and patients at the center of one of Phoenix's longest-running civic holiday traditions. The selection highlights strong institutional ties between municipal civic events and major regional medical providers ahead of the winter holiday season.

Source differences
The City of Phoenix official press release titled "Phoenix Children's® Named Grand Marshal for 39th Annual APS Electric Light Parade" provides the primary announcement and schedule details. Local outlets including Daily Independent (YourValley.net) under the headline "Phoenix Children's named grand marshal for 39th annual APS" and Signals AZ under "Phoenix APS Electric Light Parade Names Grand Marshal" republished the municipal release with identical date and venue facts.

Still uncertain
Specific parade float designs, exact street closure timelines prior to the 5:00 PM start, and which individual hospital representatives or patients will lead the procession have not yet been detailed.
