Hotai is holding its own target at 165,000 units, a 38.4% market share, across its Toyota, Lexus and Hino brands, while watching how AI-industry swings move the stock market.

What happened
CNA reporter Zhong Rongfeng writes from Taipei that Weng, Hotai's spokesman, confirmed the 430,000-unit forecast for total Taiwan new-car registrations this year. Hotai distributes three brands — Toyota, Lexus and Hino — and its own sales target stays at 165,000 units, implying a market share of about 38.4%. Weng added that the company is watching the impact of the artificial intelligence industry on stock market volatility.

Why it matters
Holding the forecast steady in a volatile year is itself the news: Hotai is not cutting its outlook even as AI-linked shares swing. With nearly four in ten new cars in Taiwan passing through Hotai's brands, its 165,000-unit target is a proxy for consumer demand across the whole market. The AI caveat is a reminder that car demand and equity sentiment now move in the same weather.

Sources agree-disagree
Only CNA has reported this in the feed, so the forecast figures have no second source. The numbers are internally consistent: 165,000 out of 430,000 is 38.4%.

Still uncertain
The feed gives no breakdown of the 165,000-unit target by brand, no monthly sales data behind the forecast, and no detail on what kind of AI impact Weng expects — whether on supply chains, consumer confidence, or share prices. The forecast is a company view, not a market consensus.
