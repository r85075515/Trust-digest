10:T977,「What happened」
Paraguay's former Minister of Economy and Finance Carlos Fernandez Valdovinos (卡洛斯・費爾南德斯・瓦爾多維諾斯), 61, surrendered to authorities in Asuncion on Friday, November 27, 2026 after the public prosecutor's office issued an arrest warrant in connection with the collapse of Ueno Bank. According to the arrest warrant reviewed by AFP, prosecutors accused the former minister of alleged acts of administration for personal gain, aggravated passive bribery, criminal association, and money laundering, which remain unverified allegations pending court adjudication. Prosecutors allege that while serving as minister in July 2025, he approved transferring tens of millions of USD in pension fund money to Ueno Bank, and after leaving office in August signed a multi-million-USD consulting contract with the business group that controlled the lender.

「Why it matters」
Ueno Bank's insolvency crisis and central bank seizure have triggered sweeping political and judicial fallout in Paraguay, including the detention of former President Santiago Pena (聖地牙哥・潘尼亞) and former central bank officials. The arrest of the former economy chief extends the criminal investigation directly into the fiscal and pension-fund decisions preceding the bank's failure.

「Source differences」
The BNN Bloomberg report titled "Paraguay ex-minister arrested in latest bank failure fallout" details the four charges listed in the AFP-reviewed arrest warrant and the alleged link between the July 2025 pension fund transfer and the post-office consulting contract. The Arab News article titled "Paraguay ex-minister arrested in latest bank failure fallout" notes that the 61-year-old former minister turned himself in on Friday and places his detention alongside the arrest of former President Santiago Pena. The Xinhua report titled "Paraguay's former economy minister surrenders to police in Ueno Bank case" confirms his surrender to police in Asuncion on November 27.

「Still uncertain」
Formal court rulings on the four criminal charges against Fernandez Valdovinos, his defense's legal response to the alleged consulting contract, and whether pre-trial detention will be ordered remain pending.
