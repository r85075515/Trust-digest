What happened
Wired reports on Kora, a desktop OS built specifically for AI enthusiasts who run local AI models. The system is the work of Cyanogen's cofounder, and it is presented as a next-generation alternative to the two dominant desktop platforms — Windows and macOS — aimed at power users rather than the mainstream.

Why it matters
Local AI is growing: running models on your own machine offers privacy, avoids subscription costs and cuts latency. An operating system tuned for that workload could make local AI easier to set up and use. But desktop operating systems are a graveyard of challengers, and Kora will have to prove it can win users away from platforms with decades of software and habit behind them.
