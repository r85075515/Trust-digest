10:T6be,What happened
Swiss energy company Alpiq submitted a formal planning approval application on November 27, 2026, to build a 300 MW / 1,200 MWh (1.2 GWh) battery energy storage system (BESS) in Niedergösgen, located in Switzerland's canton of Solothurn. The filing follows a pre-connection agreement signed with national transmission system operator Swissgrid, with construction targeted to begin in 2027 and commercial commissioning slated for 2029.

Why it matters
At four hours of storage duration (300 MW power capacity and 1.2 GWh energy capacity), the Niedergösgen facility will rank among Switzerland's largest grid-scale battery installations. It is designed to absorb intermittent solar and wind generation and provide frequency regulation and grid stabilization services across Central Europe's interconnected power network.

Source differences
Energy-Storage.News ("Alpiq seeks approval for 1.2GWh BESS in Switzerland") confirms the November 27 planning approval filing in Niedergösgen, canton of Solothurn. Battery-Tech.net ("Alpiq Seeks Approval for 1.2 GWh Swiss BESS Project") reports the same 300 MW / 1,200 MWh technical specifications and 2029 commissioning timeline, while Alpiq's corporate media release ("Alpiq advances plans for a 300 MW battery energy storage system in") details the prior grid pre-connection milestone signed with Swissgrid.

Still uncertain
Total capital expenditure for the 1.2 GWh installation, selected battery cell chemistry suppliers, and the expected regulatory review timeline for local canton approval have not yet been disclosed.
