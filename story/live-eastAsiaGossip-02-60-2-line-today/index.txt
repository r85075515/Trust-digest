10:T785,Taiwanese variety-show host Hu Gua has quit the YouTube channel Next One, which had about 600,000 subscribers, after a buyback dispute with his partner.

What happened
Hu announced at a press conference on November 19 that he is leaving the channel he ran for more than two years and starting over with a new one. His partner reportedly asked NT$20 million for Hu to buy the show back, and Hu refused, leaving the original channel behind. Within 24 hours the old channel's subscriber count fell to 562,000 — a drop of about 38,000 — while his new channel, New Next One, surged past 141,000 subscribers. Senior entertainer Andy has also spoken publicly about the split, and Ding Rouan has spoken about the painful background of the split, according to the feed headlines.

Why it matters
The numbers show the audience is moving with the host: a 38,000-subscriber loss on one side and a 141,000-subscriber gain on the other within a day make this a dispute over who owns the audience, not just a channel name. The NT$20 million price tag is now the central fact in that fight.

Sources agree / disagree
ETtoday, Yahoo News, the Liberty Times, and the China Times all carry the same core facts: the 600,000-subscriber channel, the NT$20 million buyback demand, and the November 19 announcement. The 562,000 and 141,000 figures come from ETtoday's follow-up count and are consistent with the reported 38,000-subscriber drop.

Still uncertain
The snippets do not say what Andy actually said, what Ding said in detail, or who legally owns the original channel and its back catalogue. The feed also bundles a separate item: Hu is set to return to FTV for Lunar New Year's Eve and has challenged Bai Bingbing and Chen Meifeng to a contest, with details still to come.
