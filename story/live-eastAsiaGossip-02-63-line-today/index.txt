What happened
The 63rd Golden Horse Awards were held on November 22 at the Taipei Pop Music Center. Liu Kuan-ting won Best Actor for Shuangxi (《雙囍》), which took four Golden Horse awards in total. At the after-party he shared that he had collected five celebrity autographs in one night, and that backstage he was reminded he had forgotten to thank his wife Sun Ke-fang (孫可芳) in his speech, so he called her live on the spot. Best Picture went to Shan Nan Xin Nu (《善男信女》), which battled Shuangxi (《雙囍》) through three rounds of voting before winning narrowly. The jury, led by Sylvia Chang (張艾嘉), finished voting by just after 3pm, unusually efficient. Yuan Liling (袁澧林) won Best Actress for The Fourth Act (《第四幕》).
Why it matters
Liu's speech is the story: a working actor with two trophies already, saying out loud that the doubt never stops, is the kind of honesty awards nights rarely produce. The close Best Picture race matters too, a three-round fight between two films shows a jury genuinely split, not a coronation. The takeaway is that this year's Golden Horse rewarded craft over consensus, with the night's biggest winner still sounding unsure of himself.
