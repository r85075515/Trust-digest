It is the first time the court has reached this conclusion for an Upper House election.

What happened
CNA reports, citing foreign media, that in the July 2025 Upper House election the number of voters represented by each elected member varied by as much as 3.13 times between constituencies. The Supreme Court's Grand Bench ruled today that this imbalance in vote value is in a state of unconstitutionality, the first such ruling for an Upper House election.

Why it matters
A vote in one constituency counting up to three times as much as a vote in another strikes at the equal-value principle behind elections, so a top-court ruling puts direct pressure on the Diet to redraw the balance. What happens next depends on the Diet's response, which is now the event to watch.
