FDA Approves First Drug Shown to Help Patients with Infantile-Onset Niemann-Pick Disease Type C Live Longer. The latest approval represents a large market opportunity for a drug Roche has touted to become a top-selling, multibillion-dollar product. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration today approved the first medicine for treating limb-girdle muscular dystrophy type 2I/R9 (LGMD2I/R9), a rare inherited muscle disease, for adults and pediatric patients weighing at least 30 kg. The FDA today approved Wilserdly (giredestrant), used alone after surgery in adults with estrogen receptor–positive, HER2-negative breast cancer — the most common subtype of the disease. FDA today approved Infilbray (venglustat) tablets, the first treatment for the neurological symptoms of Type 3 Gaucher disease (GD3) in adults and pediatric patients weighing at least 15 kg (approximately 33 pounds).
