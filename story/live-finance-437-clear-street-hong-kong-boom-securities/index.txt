10:T796,What happened
On November 27, 2026, the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region announced that New York-headquartered fintech firm Clear Street has established its first licensed presence in Hong Kong after completing the acquisition of Boom Securities (H.K.) Limited. Following the transaction, Boom Securities operates under the brand "Boom Securities powered by Clear Street."

Why it matters
Founded in 1997, Boom Securities provides an established local retail brokerage footprint and regulatory license in Hong Kong, while Clear Street brings cloud-native clearing, custody, and prime brokerage infrastructure. Combining both operations allows Clear Street to link retail and institutional capital markets services in Hong Kong as a hub for Asia-Pacific expansion.

Source differences
The HKSAR Government press release titled "Leading US fintech Clear Street establishes first licensed presence in Hong Kong to drive Asia-Pacific expansion (with photo)" (2026-11-27) highlights the November 27 opening announcement and rebranding to "Boom Securities powered by Clear Street." Bastille Post's report titled "Clear Street Establishes First Licensed Presence in Hong Kong After Boom Securities Acquisition" (2026-11-27) mirrors the HKSAR Government press release details. LeapRate's article titled "Clear Street Completes Boom Securities HK Acquisition | LeapRate" (2026-11-20) notes that the underlying acquisition deal itself closed on November 19, 2026, prior to the formal Hong Kong office launch announcement.

Still uncertain
Neither the HKSAR Government release nor the industry reports disclose the financial purchase price of Boom Securities or specific headcount targets for the new Hong Kong office.
