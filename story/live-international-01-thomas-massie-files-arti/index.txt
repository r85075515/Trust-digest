What happened
Massie filed articles accusing Hegseth of 'high crimes and misdemeanors,' including waging war against Iran in violation of the War Powers Resolution. Massie has said there is a 'high probability' Hegseth will not make it to the end of the year. The effort is expected to fail, but Massie wants to push President Trump to fire Hegseth before Democrats can launch their own impeachment process next year.
Why it matters
If the vote happens, it would mark the first impeachment of a Cabinet secretary during the second Trump administration — and a signal of what is to come when Democrats take control of Congress next year. The more immediate point is intra-party: a Republican is forcing the vote, not the opposition. The takeaway is that the Iran war is now a live impeachment issue in the House, whether or not the articles pass.
