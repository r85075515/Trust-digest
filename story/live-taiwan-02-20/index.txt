10:T71e,Taipei's push to relocate Songshan Airport is running into the central government's timeline: at least 20 years for a science park, says the interior minister.
What happened
Taipei Mayor Chiang Wan-an announced a plan to relocate Songshan Airport and build a Taipei AI park. Reporting to the city council, Chiang said the relocated site could be planned as a 110-hectare Taipei central park with six zones, urging the central government to start now with no time to debate feasibility. Interior Minister Liu Shih-fang said the National Airborne Service Corps' Songshan base currently has no alternative site, and that getting a science park from land acquisition through environmental review takes at least 20 years at the fastest. In a separate exchange, DPP legislator Su Chiao-hui asked during questioning that police and firefighters operating drones — helpful for public safety but a burden on personnel — get raises or bonuses; Liu said drone operation will be a basic skill in the future and that whether it qualifies for a professional-skill allowance "can be considered."
Why it matters
The airport relocation is a decades-scale land-use fight between the city, which wants the land, and the central government, which operates the airbase and controls the science-park pipeline. The 20-year figure sets the terms of the debate: this is not a mayoral-term project.
Sources agree / disagree
All items come from CNA's politics desk, reporting Chiang's council remarks, Liu's 20-year response, and the drone-allowance exchange. No conflicts appear; the "20 vs 110" figures refer to different things (years vs hectares).
Still uncertain
The snippets say nothing about funding, the AI park's actual scope, or any central-government commitment to start.
