Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC) announced that its next-generation 1.4nm-class A14 process node is now ready for customer chip design following the release of version 0.9 of its Process Design Kit (PDK) and design ecosystem tools, as detailed at its 2026 Europe Open Innovation Platform (OIP) Ecosystem Workshop in Munich. According to TSMC, A14 introduces Boost Cells and Low-Power Cells, delivering either a 14% performance uplift at the same power or a 23% power reduction at the same speed compared with its N2P node. TSMC is targeting late 2027 for A14 risk production ahead of subsequent high-volume manufacturing.

為什麼重要
Releasing the v0.9 PDK marks a critical engineering milestone that allows leading AI accelerator, smartphone SoC, and high-performance computing customers to begin physical layout and silicon tape-out planning for sub-2nm chips.

來源異同
Tom's Hardware, DIGITIMES, and MLQ.ai all consistently report the availability of the A14 v0.9 PDK for customer design-in, the 14% performance gain or 23% power reduction relative to N2P, and the late-2027 risk production schedule.

仍不確定
Which flagship customers will secure the initial A14 wafer capacity in 2027–2028 and whether backside power delivery will be introduced on the baseline A14 variant or reserved for a subsequent A14P derivative remain to be confirmed.
