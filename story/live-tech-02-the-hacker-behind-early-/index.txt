Kurtaj, 21, had leaked 90 videos of Grand Theft Auto VI in 2022, years before the game's public release last week.
What happened
Kurtaj pleaded guilty to the Rockstar Games hack and to other hacks he carried out when he was a teenager, including hacks of Uber and Revolut. After hacking Rockstar, he leaked 90 videos of Grand Theft Auto VI in 2022, well before the game reached the public. The Verge reports that he was sentenced to seven years in prison but released on licence, and the BBC reports the same outcome, with a headline describing the release on licence and a warning not to let this happen again. The feed material does not reproduce the warning in full or say who gave it.
Why it matters
The case matters because the 2022 leak exposed unreleased material from one of the most anticipated games of the decade, years before its public release. The sentence and the release on licence together show how the court treated a serious hacking case carried out by a teenager, and the outcome is likely to be read as a signal about the consequences of leaking unreleased work. It also matters for the companies affected, whose internal systems and unreleased material were the targets of the hacks.
Sources agree / disagree
The Verge and BBC Technology agree on the central facts: Kurtaj pleaded guilty, leaked 90 videos of Grand Theft Auto VI in 2022, was sentenced to seven years in prison, and was released on licence. The figures in the headlines that look different, 90 and 6, are not a conflict: 90 is the number of leaked videos, and 6 is part of the game's title, Grand Theft Auto VI.
