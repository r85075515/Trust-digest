10:T5ea,Four construction workers died in a workplace accident at TSMC's Kaohsiung fab construction site on the evening of October 20, prompting TSMC to voluntarily suspend steel-structure work at the site and at all new fab sites across Taiwan.
What happened
According to CNA (reporter Chang Chien-hsin, Hsinchu, Oct. 21), the accident occurred at the TSMC Kaohsiung site in Nanzi District on the evening of the 20th. Workers were performing steel-structure hoisting and installation when they fell from height, killing four workers. TSMC said steel-structure work is voluntarily suspended both at the Kaohsiung site and at all new fab construction sites island-wide. Related work has been halted, and the Southern Taiwan Science Park Bureau has dispatched personnel to investigate the cause.
Why it matters
A fatal accident at Taiwan's most strategically important construction program triggers an immediate safety stand-down: suspending steel work at every new site signals TSMC is treating this as a systemic risk, not a single-site incident, and regulators are now formally involved.
Sources agree / disagree
CNA's business desk and PTS News Network report the same core facts: four deaths, a fall from height during steel-structure work at the Kaohsiung site on the 20th, and the voluntary suspension. No conflicts appear in the snippets.
Still uncertain
