10:T61a,A federal judge has barred the Trump administration from closing East Potomac Park this weekend, ruling the closure cannot go forward for at least another two weeks. The decision is a setback for President Trump, who said the park would close on Sunday so construction could begin on a new golf course.
What happened
The judge ordered a two-week delay to any work at the historic Washington public golf course, not far from the White House. The plan would close the park's existing course so an 18-hole, championship-length course designed by architect Tom Fazio could be built. The ruling is the latest development in a months-long legal challenge to the president's plans for East Potomac Golf Links.
Why it matters
East Potomac Park is public land with a historic course, and the fight over it has become a test of how far a president can go in remaking a public park. A two-week pause does not settle the case, but it stops a closure that would have been hard to reverse once construction began.
