What happened
CNA reported from Taipei on October 29. Scholars cited by CNA said adding Zhang Shengmin broke a record of no Politburo additions at a Central Committee plenary session since 1994 and said its purpose was to reassure the military and end the absence of a military representative. A Liberty Times item carried a different headline about the communique and a scholar view about Xi Jinping's position, but its summary was truncated after a communique quotation attributed to Xinhua, so its full argument was not stated.
Why it matters
The appointment matters directly because it restores a military representative to the Politburo after the absence described by the scholars. The CNA reading treats the addition as reassurance to the military rather than a routine personnel change, and the break with practice since 1994 gives that reading its weight.
Sources agree / disagree
Two outlets covered the same plenum with different angles: CNA focused on Zhang Shengmin and the military representative reading, while Liberty Times headlined a different interpretation of the communique. Trust label: Bundled stories — two outlets reported distinct angles on the same plenum, not the same corroborated claim about the appointment.
Still uncertain
The missing key information is Zhang Shengmin's role and background, the identities of the scholars cited, the full communique wording, and the evidence behind the Liberty Times headline reading.
