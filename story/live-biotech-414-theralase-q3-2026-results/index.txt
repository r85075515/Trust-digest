10:T83d,What happened
On November 27, 2026, Toronto-based clinical-stage pharmaceutical firm Theralase Technologies Inc. (TSXV: TLT; OTCQB: TLTFF) published its unaudited condensed interim consolidated financial statements for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. Cash rose to C$7,204,200 (up from C$182,914 at year-end 2025) and working capital reached C$7,907,730 following roughly C$9.7 million in net private placement proceeds, while nine-month net loss narrowed 12% year-over-year to C$3,013,858 on revenue of C$527,506. Alongside the financials, the company reported interim Phase II non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) trial data showing 55 of 91 evaluable patients (60.4%) achieved a complete response at any point.

Why it matters
Strengthening liquidity while cutting nine-month drug-division R&D expenses by 26% extends Theralase's operational runway as it advances its lead energy-activated small-molecule bladder cancer therapy toward potential 2027 regulatory submissions. However, management explicitly noted that additional financing will still be required to sustain ongoing clinical development.

Source differences
The company's Newsfile Corp. release carried on TradingView ("Theralase(R) Releases Q32026 Financial Statements") details the headline cash balance, 12% loss reduction, and an upcoming investor conference call scheduled for December 9, 2026. Stockhouse ("Theralase reports C$7.2 million cash and interim bladder cancer data") breaks down exact line-item figures—including the August 24, 2026 brokered private placement of 14,812,500 units at C$0.24 per unit—and pairs the balance sheet update with the 55-of-91 patient complete response rate.

Still uncertain
How durable the 60.4% complete response rate proves at 12- and 15-month follow-up intervals, and the timing and dilution terms of the next capital raise flagged by management, remain undetermined.
