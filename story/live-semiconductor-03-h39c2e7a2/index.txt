What happened
CNA, reporting from London on November 23, said European efforts to ensure key digital services operate under European control are gradually showing results. Governments and regulated industries now treat digital sovereignty as a consideration when procuring cloud services, the report said. TechNews carried a closely matching headline the same day, describing the same momentum in Europe's sovereign cloud push. Both outlets agree that US hyperscalers still dominate the market, so the shift is one of procurement priorities rather than a change in who supplies most cloud capacity.
Why it matters
Procurement choices by governments and regulated industries are the lever Europe has to build a sovereign cloud market. If sovereignty criteria harden into contract requirements, European providers gain a real opening; if they stay a preference, the incumbents keep their lead.
