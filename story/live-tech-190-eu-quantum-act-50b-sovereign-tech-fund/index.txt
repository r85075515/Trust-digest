10:T6bf,The European Commission formally unveiled its legislative proposal for the EU Quantum Act (COM(2026) 965) alongside plans for a Sovereign Tech Equity Fund aimed at mobilizing 50 billion euros (54.73 billion U.S. dollars) in public and private equity financing across quantum technologies and the broader AI stack. Executive Vice-President for Tech Sovereignty, Security and Democracy Henna Virkkunen said the regulation will establish a Quantum Europe Framework Initiative requiring member states to align national strategies from research through industrial scale-up by 2030.

Designed to close Europe's commercialization gap and reduce reliance on non-EU critical component suppliers, the proposal mandates coordinated action across seven operational pillars—including large-scale quantum infrastructure, SME financing access, talent attraction, and post-quantum cryptography (PQC) migration. The Sovereign Tech Equity Fund will combine EU budget, European Investment Bank (EIB), and member-state capital to crowd in private institutional equity.

Official European Commission releases, Reuters (via Euronext), and Quantum Computing Report all agree on the 50 billion euro equity fund target, the Quantum Europe Framework Initiative structure, and the 2030 industrial leadership goal, while minor dollar conversions vary between 54.73 billion and 54.85 billion U.S. dollars due to exchange-rate rounding.

The proposal must now undergo legislative review and amendment by the European Parliament and the Council of the European Union before entering into force.
