What happened
The Decoder reports Google wants Argon to be "the only AI model robots need." The model is being opened to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers. But the published example prompts suggest the demos still lean heavily on human guidance — the autonomy story is ahead of the evidence so far.
Why it matters
The judgment call is simple: whoever owns the default robot brain owns the next platform. Google is moving first with an all-in-one pitch, but the gap between the benchmark score and the hand-held demos is where competitors will attack.
Sources agree / disagree
Google AI Blog (the announcement) and The Decoder (the analysis) agree on the capabilities and the 87% ERQA figure. Trust label: Single source on independent verification — ERQA is Google's own benchmark. The Science item on the Riemann hypothesis in this cluster is unrelated noise.
Still uncertain
Real-world deployment performance is unverified, and pricing/availability details are thin.
