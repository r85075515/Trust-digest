What happened
The combined profit of the three financial sectors reached NT$1,271.544 billion in the first three quarters, growing 77.5 percent year on year. CNA reports that the figure not only set a new high for the same period but also pushed higher a full-year profit record that had already been broken in August. Two drivers are named in the report: this year's capital market conditions, and bank lending that lifted net interest income. The available summary does not break the total down by banking, insurance or securities, and it does not give the individual sector figures behind the combined result.
Why it matters
A 77.5 percent annual increase to a record level shows the scale of the improvement across the financial industry as a whole, rather than in one sector alone. Because the nine-month total has already exceeded a full-year record broken in August, the remaining quarter starts from a historically high base. The drivers named also matter for interpretation: market conditions supporting the result may fluctuate, while lending-driven net interest income is tied to bank loan volumes, so the two sources of growth carry different kinds of sensitivity going forward.
