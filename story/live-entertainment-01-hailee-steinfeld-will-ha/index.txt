10:T66b,Singer and actress Hailee Steinfeld is bringing a little extra magic to her role in Disney's new animated feature Hexed. In addition to leading the voice cast, she contributes a cover of The Cars' hit Magic, released Friday ahead of the movie's theatrical premiere on November 25. Her version keeps all of the 1980s flair of The Cars' original.
What happened
The cover is a double duty: Steinfeld is both the film's voice lead and the voice behind its tie-in single. Billboard's preview stresses the continuity between the two jobs, the same performer carrying the film's sound from the screen to the single. The release timing, a Friday drop weeks before the premiere, is designed to let the song build an audience the film can then inherit.
Why it matters
Animated films live or die on whether audiences arrive already humming something. A recognizable song, sung by the film's own star, is the cheapest marketing a studio can buy: every stream is a trailer that plays itself. If Magic catches on, Hexed opens with a head start; if it does not, the film must earn its audience from scratch.
