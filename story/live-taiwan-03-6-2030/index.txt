What happened
Central News Agency (CNA), reporting from Taipei on November 26, said Copenhagen Infrastructure Partners (CIP) announced today that it had started an onshore substation engineering, procurement and construction contract with TECO Electric and Machinery. CIP Asia-Pacific regional president Hsu Nai-wen (許乃文) said the total construction cost of Fengmiao II exceeds NT$160 billion, and the project aims to complete a NT$140 billion refinancing plan before the end of June next year. Hsu described the signing with TECO as a strong reassurance for the project. The headline target attached to the partnership is connection to the grid in 2030.

Why it matters
The figures show the scale of financing still to be secured relative to the project's total cost, with the refinancing target close to the overall construction budget. Locking in the onshore substation work is a concrete construction step, because offshore generation cannot deliver power onshore without that infrastructure. The stated 2030 grid-connection goal also gives investors, suppliers and the energy authorities a timetable against which later financing and construction milestones can be judged.
