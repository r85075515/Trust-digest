10:T810,South Korean actor Ko Kyung-pyo (高庚杓), 36, was caught in a suspected drink-driving incident in the early hours of Nov. 14 in Seoul's Mapo District, when his car hit a cleaning truck. Police breathalysed him at the scene and found his blood alcohol level met the standard for licence revocation. The case is still under investigation, and his agency has apologised.

What happened
Ko was driving in Mapo District in the early hours of Nov. 14 when his car collided with a cleaning truck. Police arrived at the scene and carried out a breath test, which showed his blood alcohol concentration had reached the level that triggers licence revocation. His agency has admitted the incident and apologised. The case remains under investigation, and no further findings have been reported in the feed summaries.

Why it matters
The incident could ripple through several of Ko's projects. Headlines warn it may affect Park Bo-gum's (朴寶劍) new programme, the second season of The Country Barber (鄉村魔髮師2), and The Great Escape (大逃脫), while a fan meeting due in seven days may be called off and a variety show involving Yoo Jae-suk (劉在錫) also faces questions. One variety show has already said it will cut Ko's footage as much as possible to avoid making viewers uncomfortable.

Sources agree and disagree
ETtoday, KSD and Yahoo agree on the core facts: the early-morning crash in Mapo District, the cleaning truck, and a breath-test result at licence-revocation level. The figures 36 and 2 that appear across the headlines are not a conflict: 36 is Ko's age, while 2 is the season number of The Country Barber 2.

Still uncertain
The feed summaries give no exact blood alcohol reading, no detail on the crash or any injuries, and no word on whether Ko will face charges. How his agency, broadcasters and the affected productions will respond — and whether the fan meeting goes ahead — remains to be confirmed.
