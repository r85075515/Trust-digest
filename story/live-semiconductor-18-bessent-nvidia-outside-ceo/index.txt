What happened: Jensen Huang (黃仁勳) stepped down as president, CEO and director on Thursday (Nov 12) after revelations of extramarital affairs, including one involving Alice Huang (黃樂), a businesswoman US officials suspect of working for Chinese intelligence - a scandal this site has tracked through 17 cards. CFO Colette Kress (克雷斯) is interim CEO and Harvey C. Jones (瓊斯) chairs the board's search, which the company said would consider internal and external candidates. Reuters also reported, citing the Financial Times, that the board has approached Arm Holdings CEO Rene Haas (哈斯) about the job.

Why it matters: A sitting Treasury secretary publicly steering the succession of the world's most important AI chipmaker - and attaching a 10-day deadline on export-compliance answers - moves the Huang scandal from a corporate governance crisis into direct government pressure. Still uncertain: Whether the board will follow Bessent's steer or treat it as political pressure; what exactly the government wants answered about export-compliance culture and what happens if the 10 days pass; whether Haas or another outsider is a serious candidate; and Nvidia's position, which had not been stated in the coverage reviewed.
