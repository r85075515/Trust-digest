What happened
Trump played down concerns about the outages in an interview with Fox News's Jacqui Heinrich in Shenzhen, China, describing them as individual events and saying he would let the country know when it is time to panic. The Hill and TMZ both carried the "relax" message. The same batch also includes a BBC report on whether Trump would be keen to meet North Korea's leader Kim Jong Un in China.

Why it matters
A president's public reassurance is meant to stop panic buying, but calling the outages individual events only holds if investigations show they are genuinely unrelated. If a systemic cause emerges, the remark becomes a point of accountability instead.

Sources agree / disagree
The Hill and TMZ agree on the core message — stockpiling is unnecessary and the outages are individual events — while the BBC item in the same batch covers a different subject, a possible meeting with Kim Jong Un, and should not be read as part of the outage story.

Still uncertain
The feed material gives no count, locations or causes for the outages and no full transcript of the interview, so whether the outages really are isolated incidents awaits investigation.
