Death threats against an elected official over an endorsement cross from political disagreement into personal safety, and a request for Marshals protection signals that the threats were treated as serious. The case also shows the intensity of the Michigan Senate contest, where a Democratic congresswoman's endorsement of the Republican candidate was bound to provoke a strong reaction inside her own party. The underlying political dispute, which candidate Stevens supports, remains separate from the safety question her request raises.
