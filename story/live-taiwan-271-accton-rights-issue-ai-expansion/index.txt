Taiwanese networking equipment manufacturer Accton Technology (stock code 2345) announced on November 27, 2026 that it set the subscription price for its cash capital increase at 1,217 NTD per share. Upon completion, the offering will raise 12.17 billion NTD to support capacity expansion and replenish working capital. On the same day, Accton shares closed down 65 NTD at 1,555 NTD, putting the offering price at an approximate 21.7% discount. Management also outlined plans to invest 32 billion NTD across 2026 and 2027 to expand production across Taiwan, Vietnam, Singapore, Malaysia, and the United States.

「Why it matters」
Global demand for AI data center switches, systems, and rack-level networking infrastructure continues to drive capital expenditure among hardware suppliers. Raising 12.17 billion NTD in equity financing while deploying 32 billion NTD across five jurisdictions underscores how Taiwanese server and networking suppliers are simultaneously scaling AI manufacturing capacity and diversifying geographic supply chain footprints.

Both reports agree on the 1,217 NTD issue price and 12.17 billion NTD total proceeds.

「Still uncertain」
