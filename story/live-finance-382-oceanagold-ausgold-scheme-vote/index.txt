10:T5cf,What happened
On Nov. 27, 2026, shareholders of Ausgold Limited voted 99.40% in favor of a scheme of arrangement under which OceanaGold Corporation will acquire 100% of Ausgold's issued shares. Under the terms of the transaction, Ausgold shareholders will receive 0.03365 common shares of OceanaGold for each Ausgold share held. The transaction remains pending and is subject to court approval before closing.

Why it matters
The near-unanimous shareholder endorsement removes a major corporate governance hurdle for OceanaGold's all-share consolidation of Ausgold's gold assets, advancing the merger toward its final judicial sign-off.

Source differences
The primary corporate release carried by PR Newswire under the headline "OceanaGold Announces Ausgold Shareholder Approval" detailed the 99.40% approval tally, the Nov. 27 Scheme Meeting date, and the 0.03365 share exchange ratio. Investing News Network republished the announcement under the identical headline "OceanaGold Announces Ausgold Shareholder Approval", while Newsfilter indexed the release as "OceanaGold Announces Ausgold Shareholder Approval" noting the pending court approval condition.

Still uncertain
The exact date of the final court hearing to approve the scheme and the subsequent timeline for share settlement and delisting of Ausgold have not yet been finalized.
