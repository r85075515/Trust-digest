What happened

Why it matters
Handheld PC gaming is in a price war, and a $580 cut on a 32GB/1TB SteamOS device rewrites the value math against Valve's Deck OLED. The RTX 50-series discounts matter too — current-gen gaming laptops rarely see $900 cuts outside major sales events, which makes this week's list unusually aggressive for mid-October.
