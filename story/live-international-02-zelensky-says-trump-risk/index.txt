What happened
President Volodymyr Zelensky warned that Donald Trump risks a “historic mistake” if he backs Russia. Trump defended his decision to waive the Russian sanctions on Sunday, writing in a Truth Social post that it keeps Moscow at the negotiating table, amid backlash from both sides of the aisle. Separately, Zelensky pressed Sunday for more information on possible restrictions to Starshield, a US-provided satellite internet service used by the Ukrainian military.
Why it matters
Sanctions are Washington's main economic leverage over Moscow, so waiving them days before they bite — over the objections of both parties — reframes the US posture from pressure to courtship. Zelensky's “historic mistake” language is aimed at locking in the cost of that shift, while the Starshield question shows Kyiv is also worried about the tools it fights with, not just the diplomacy around it.
Sources agree / disagree
The BBC, the NYT, The Hill and Bloomberg agree on the core sequence — sanctions blocked/waived, Trump's negotiating-table rationale, Zelensky's warning. The framing splits: the NYT and Bloomberg foreground Zelensky's alarm (“historic mistake,” “pushing Ukraine to yield”), while the BBC and The Hill foreground Trump's defense.
