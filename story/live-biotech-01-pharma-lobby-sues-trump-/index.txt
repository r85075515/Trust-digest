The lawsuit targets the GLOBE rule, whose final version was published last week and folds a most-favored-nation pricing system — benchmarked to what a group of comparable countries pay — into the Medicare Part B program.
What happened
The GLOBE rule intends to set hospital drug prices for Medicare patients. Its final version, published last week, spells out exactly how the most-favored-nation pricing system integrates into Medicare Part B. The pharma lobby's lawsuit challenges that framework in court. The other item in this cluster, a STAT News newsletter signup page, contains no reporting on the suit.
Why it matters
The judgment is simple: if the rule survives, US hospital drug prices get benchmarked to cheaper foreign prices and hospital bills change; if the court kills it, the pricing status quo holds. Either way, this is the legal fight that decides whether most-favored-nation pricing has a future in American health care.
