Under U.S. Commerce Department rules cited in the reports, eligible companies expanding in the United States can import semiconductors free of Section 232 duties up to 2.5 times their planned U.S. production capacity during the approved construction period, and up to 1.5 times capacity after project completion. Focus Taiwan, Taipei Times, and Liberty Times report consistent details from Cheng's press conference, noting that the bilateral investment memorandum and trade agreements will be submitted to the Legislative Yuan once U.S. administrative procedures conclude.
