10:T5cf,CNET Deals is offering lifetime access to a bundle of AI tools for $70 through 1min.AI ahead of Black Friday, removing the monthly fee from tools buyers already use.

What happened
The 1min.AI deal unlocks ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and more, according to the roundup, with no more monthly fees. The same CNET Deals coverage also promotes other early Black Friday offers: preorders for the Lectric XP Lite 3 and discounts on Lectric's current inventory with freebies, savings of up to $840 on a Lectric e-bike, and up to $1,100 off select xTool machines, pitched for starting a side hustle or upgrading a creative workspace with laser cutters and more.

Why it matters
The real question is not the headline price but the definition of lifetime. A $70 one-time payment only saves money if the tools, usage limits and the platform itself last, and if the models buyers want stay inside the bundle.

Sources agree-disagree
All three items come from the same outlet, CNET Deals, and they describe different products, so their different figures are not a conflict: $840 belongs to the Lectric e-bike deal and $1,100 to xTool machines, while $70 belongs to 1min.AI. Readers should not compare those numbers as if they priced the same thing.

Still uncertain
The material provided does not spell out usage caps, which AI models or tiers are included, or what happens if 1min.AI changes its terms. Those terms decide whether the deal is a bargain or a locked-in limit.
