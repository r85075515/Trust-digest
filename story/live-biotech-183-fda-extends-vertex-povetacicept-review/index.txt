10:T637,Vertex Pharmaceuticals disclosed that the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) extended the Prescription Drug User Fee Act (PDUFA) target action date for povetacicept, an investigational biologic for adults with IgA nephropathy (IgAN), from November 30, 2026, to no later than February 28, 2027. The FDA classified Vertex's response to a late-cycle agency information request as a major amendment, triggering a standard three-month extension so reviewers can evaluate the additional submission.

Povetacicept is seeking accelerated approval based on a week-36 interim analysis of the Phase 3 RAINIER trial, with Vertex planning a once-every-four-weeks home auto-injector launch if cleared. The delay pushes Vertex behind approved IgAN competitors Otsuka's Voyxact and Vera Therapeutics' Trutakna, sending Vertex shares down roughly 6% to 10% in November 27 trading while lifting Vera Therapeutics stock by about 17%.

Vertex's official release, Reuters, and Unusual Whales all agree on the February 28, 2027 deadline, the original November 30, 2026 target date, and the major-amendment classification. Intraday percentage declines reported for Vertex varied slightly between 6% and 10.3% depending on market snapshot timing during the shortened post-Thanksgiving session.

Vertex has not disclosed the specific technical content of the FDA's late-cycle information request, and approval by the revised February 28, 2027 date remains unguaranteed.
