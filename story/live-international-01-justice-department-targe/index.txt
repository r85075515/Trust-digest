What happened
Per a DOJ press release issued Monday, the department targeted and charged 28 alleged California voters who are not citizens; 11 non-Americans residing in the country were arrested in California on Monday, among the 28 total charged in federal criminal complaints. The DOJ also filed an emergency request in federal court to stop California from processing ballots returned by people on the voter rolls the federal government describes as 'likely noncitizens.' NPR's headline frames it as election tensions escalating in California. A Science item in the same cluster — the DOJ funding studies of gender dysphoria and violent behavior — is a separate story.
Why it matters
Federal intervention in a state's ballot processing is the flashpoint: it pits DOJ voter-fraud enforcement against California's election administration, with the 'likely noncitizen' designation itself likely to be litigated.
Sources agree / disagree
