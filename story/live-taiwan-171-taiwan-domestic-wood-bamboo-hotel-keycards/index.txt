10:T5a1,Taiwan's Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency unveiled domestically manufactured wood and bamboo hotel room keycards on November 26, 2026, partnering with eight hospitality operators—including The Grand Hotel, Gloria Manor, Chateau Beach Resort Kenting, and Hotel Royal Chihpen—to phase out conventional plastic cards.

Hospitality operators in Australia and Europe have increasingly adopted wooden keycards, yet existing wooden cards in Taiwan were previously manufactured overseas. Sourcing FSC-certified domestic timber and bamboo shortens supply-chain transport distances, lowers carbon emissions, and creates commercial demand for sustainable local forestry.

Both the Forestry and Nature Conservation Agency's official release and United Daily News report that eight hotels have joined the rollout and note technical hurdles in embedding RFID chips within thin wood veneers. United Daily News adds remarks from Grand Hotel Chairwoman Yeh Chu-lan (葉菊蘭) and agency Director-General Lin Hua-ching (林華慶) regarding potential future expansion into wooden luggage tags and credit cards.

Specific adoption timelines, unit procurement volumes, and cost comparisons against standard PVC keycards across the eight participating properties have not yet been disclosed.
