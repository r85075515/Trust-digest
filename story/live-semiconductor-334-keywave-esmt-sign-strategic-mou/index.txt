10:T66d,What happened
UK-based KEYWAVE Technology Limited and Taiwan-based Elite Semiconductor Microelectronics Technology Inc. (ESMT) announced a strategic Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) to evaluate integrating KEYWAVE's low-power radar sensing chips with ESMT's Sub-GHz wireless communication solutions. Both companies stated that combining sensing and wireless connectivity will be a key driver for next-generation smart Internet of Things (AIoT) deployments.

Why it matters
Pairing low-power radar sensing with Sub-GHz radio links addresses long-range, low-power edge detection requirements in smart buildings, industrial monitoring, and ambient sensing. For ESMT—traditionally known for specialty memory ICs—the partnership expands its wireless and edge-sensing ecosystem with European radar silicon expertise.

Source differences
DIGITIMES Asia published "UK-Based KEYWAVE Technology and Taiwan's ESMT Sign Strategic MOU", quoting KEYWAVE CEO Jenny Cheng on establishing the partnership and detailing the joint evaluation of radar sensing and Sub-GHz wireless integration. DIGITIMES Chinese published "KEYWAVE攜手台灣晶豪科技簽署策略MOU 整合雷達與Sub-GHz搶攻", framing the tie-up around AIoT market expansion and highlighting KEYWAVE's low-power radar chips alongside ESMT's Sub-GHz portfolio.

Still uncertain
Specific commercial timelines, joint reference design targets, financial terms, and whether the non-binding MOU will convert into definitive supply or licensing agreements.
