10:T8e0,What happened
The Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts recommended approving a local school property tax reduction worth an estimated 354 million USD (354.1 million USD in filing figures) under the state's Jobs, Energy, Technology and Innovation (JETI) program for a proposed 22.6 billion USD semiconductor campus by Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. (TSMC) in Brazos County. Brazos County scheduled a resident briefing for December 7, followed by a College Station Independent School District public hearing on December 10 to consider designating the roughly 3,200-acre site as a reinvestment zone.

Why it matters
If finalized and selected by TSMC, the Brazos County project would mark TSMC's first advanced 2-nanometer-class wafer manufacturing campus in Texas, targeting construction in 2027–2028 and commercial production in 2031 for AI and high-performance computing chips. The recommendation underscores intensifying competition among U.S. states using multi-hundred-million-dollar local tax incentives to attract leading-edge foundry capacity.

Source differences
DIGITIMES's "Texas comptroller backs US$354 million tax break for TSMC", Manufacturing Dive's "TSMC proposes $22.6B advanced wafer facility in Texas", Tom's Hardware's "TSMC files for $22.6 billion Texas fab campus with local tax breaks", and Anue's "台積電德州廠減稅案獲州審計長支持估省3.54億美元、12月10日聽證" all align on the 22.6 billion USD proposed investment, the estimated 354 million USD tax reduction, and TSMC's ongoing evaluation between Texas and Arizona. Tom's Hardware and Anue break down the school maintenance and operations tax over 20 years (759.3 million USD without the deal versus 405.2 million USD with the deal), while Anue specifies the December 7 county briefing and December 10 school district hearing dates.

Still uncertain
Whether the College Station Independent School District and Texas governor will grant final approval after the December hearings, and whether TSMC will ultimately commit to building the Brazos County campus rather than expanding further in Arizona.
