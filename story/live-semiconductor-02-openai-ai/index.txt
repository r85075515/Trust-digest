10:T641,OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki has proposed that AI developers set shared limits on development, using perplexity as the common yardstick, and not cross the thresholds they set until monitoring and safety mechanisms meet the standard, according to Central News Agency reporting from Bengaluru on November 22.
What happened
Pachocki said AI developers should agree on common restrictions and use perplexity, a measure of how uncertain a language model is about its predictions, as the shared metric for tracking AI progress. The rule he proposes is simple: until monitoring and safety mechanisms reach the required standard, the set threshold must not be crossed. TechNews carried the same proposal under the same framing.
Why it matters
This is the concrete version of Pachocki's broader speed-limit argument: a named metric, a named condition, and a named red line. Perplexity gives labs something measurable to argue about instead of trading slogans about safety, and the do-not-cross rule gives the metric teeth. If rival labs adopt it, the industry gets a shared dashboard; if they do not, the proposal still sets the terms of the debate.
Sources agree-disagree
TechNews and Central News Agency report the same proposal with the same core facts, the same metric and the same condition, with no contradictions. Trust label: Two-source agreement.
Still uncertain
The summaries give no numeric threshold values, no timeline, and no detail on what counts as adequate monitoring or safety mechanisms.
