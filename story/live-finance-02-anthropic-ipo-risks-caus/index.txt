10:T8e6,Anthropic has set the price range for its initial public offering at $70 to $78 a share, with trading expected to begin on November 19, CNBC reports, citing sources. The Financial Times reports that bankers are preparing to raise the valuation to $1.5 trillion or more after strong investor demand, and Bloomberg carries a warning, attributed to Selby, that the listing risks triggering a correction in AI stocks.

What happened
CNBC reports the $70 to $78 range and the November 19 trading start. The Financial Times reports bankers preparing a higher valuation, and its interactive calculator invites readers to judge whether the AI company is worth $500 billion or $4 trillion. TechCrunch reports that each of the seven co-founders plans to sell exactly the same number of shares, bringing each about $250 million in cash at the midpoint of the price range while leaving the group in control of the company. Bloomberg reports Selby as saying the IPO risks causing an AI market correction. The listing is also expected to come roughly five months after Elon Musk took SpaceX public in a record offering.

Why it matters
A $1.5 trillion valuation would put a company that has never traded publicly near the top of the market by value, so the pricing decision is a live test of how far investors will stretch AI valuations. The founders' equal $250 million cash-outs show who benefits first if demand holds.

Sources agree–disagree
The outlets report different figures because they measure different things: $70 to $78 is the per-share price range, $1.5 trillion is a possible valuation, $500 billion to $4 trillion is the range in the Financial Times calculator tool, and $250 million is each founder's expected cash proceeds. None of these figures contradicts another. The earlier raw flag of 70 versus 1 compared a share price with a valuation figure and was not a real conflict.

Still uncertain
The final valuation and the exact listing price are not yet set and depend on investor demand. The feed summaries do not state what mechanism Selby links to the correction warning, or the timing and size of the SpaceX offering used as the comparison.
