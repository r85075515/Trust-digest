What happened
The October 2027 meeting will be given over to strategy rather than a vote on policy. The report describes the change as the opening move in a wider effort by Warsh to reshape the way the Federal Reserve works, rather than a one-off scheduling decision. No details of the strategy agenda were included in the short report, and no other decision from the meeting was described.

Why it matters
A policy vote is the moment when the central bank sets its stance, so removing the vote from a scheduled meeting changes what markets, lawmakers and the public can expect from that date. A strategy session in its place signals that Warsh wants the meeting used to rethink how the institution operates. The move matters because it is framed as the start of a broader overhaul, not an isolated change, which means later meetings could follow the same pattern.
