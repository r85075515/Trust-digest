On November 27, 2026, Canadian clinical-stage biotechnology firm Arch Biopartners Inc. (TSXV: ARCH; OTCQB: ACHFF) announced the closing of a non-brokered private placement offering. The company issued 1,126,353 common shares at CAD 0.85 per share, raising gross proceeds of CAD 957,400 and bringing its total issued and outstanding common shares to 69,059,642.

Why it matters
The financing provides working capital to onboard U.S. clinical trial sites for Arch Biopartners' Phase II study of its lead drug candidate, LSALT peptide, aimed at preventing or treating cardiac surgery-associated acute kidney injury, as well as advancing pre-clinical programs.

Source differences
Arch Biopartners' official press release ("Arch Biopartners Closes Non-Brokered Private Placement") and Investing.com's report ("Arch Biopartners closes $957400 private placement offering") both draw from the company's GlobeNewswire announcement, reporting identical share counts (1,126,353 shares at CAD 0.85) and total outstanding shares (69,059,642). Gosset News' article ("Arch Biopartners closes CAD 957,400 private placement to fund U.S. kidney trial sites") highlights the allocation of proceeds toward U.S. Phase II trial site onboarding and notes that final TSX Venture Exchange acceptance remains pending.

Still uncertain
The private placement remains subject to final acceptance by the TSX Venture Exchange, and the company has not disclosed specific patient enrollment timelines for the U.S. Phase II trial sites.
