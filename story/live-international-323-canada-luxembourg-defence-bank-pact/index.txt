10:T6d2,What happened
The Department of Finance Canada announced on November 27, 2026, that Finance Minister François-Philippe Champagne will travel to Luxembourg City to sign the founding Articles of Agreement of the Defence, Security and Resilience Bank (DSRB). During the visit, Champagne will also join Luxembourg Finance Minister Gilles Roth and Luxembourg Stock Exchange CEO Julie Becker for a bell-ringing ceremony at LuxSE headquarters.

Why it matters
Establishing a dedicated multilateral financing institution for defense and security infrastructure marks a major step in allied financial coordination. For Canada, signing the founding treaty reinforces transatlantic defense-industrial ties while domestic cities compete to host the new bank's permanent headquarters.

Source differences
The official Canadian government release titled "Minister Champagne will visit Luxembourg to sign the Defence" details the treaty signing and LuxSE ceremony. Traders Union's report "Canada to sign defence bank agreement in Luxembourg, deepen economic ties" and e2gold's item "Canada signs defence bank pact in Luxembourg" confirm the November 30 signing date, while policy outlet The Logic's briefing "Canada could shoulder outsized financial risk in new defence bank, C.D. Howe warns" notes analytical concerns over potential Canadian capital exposure.

Still uncertain
The exact capital subscription shares of all nine founding members, final parliamentary ratification timelines, and which Canadian city will win the headquarters bid remain undecided.
