10:T93e,Microsoft Outlook stayed down in a widespread global outage that also hit Teams and other Microsoft 365 services. Reports of users unable to reach Outlook email through the web or apps began coming in from around the world just before 1 p.m. ET on Thursday.

What happened
Since just before 1 p.m. ET on Thursday, users worldwide have reported being unable to access Outlook email, either through the web or through apps. Microsoft quickly acknowledged the issue from its Microsoft 365 Status account, saying it had determined that a portion of infrastructure was experiencing elevated load across multiple regions, which was likely contributing to the connectivity problems. Outlook and Teams were among the platforms hit by the elevated load. The company said the issue affecting Outlook, Teams and other services was not caused by malicious or cyber activity. The figures in the coverage differ: headlines from CNBC and Engadget cite Microsoft 365, while other snippets in the set carry the figure 12. The 365 is the product name, not a count of anything affected, and the feed record flags the gap without explaining it.

Why it matters
Email is how much of the working world communicates, so an Outlook outage that lasts for hours stalls decisions, meetings and customer contact across countries at once. Microsoft's quick acknowledgement and its statement ruling out malicious or cyber activity matter because they narrow the question to capacity and infrastructure rather than security. With no restoration time given, users and employers were left planning around an outage with no end in sight.

Sources agree / disagree
Six outlets — CNBC, The Verge, Engadget, Financial Times, Hollywood Life and NPR News — covered the outage across seven feed items, and they agree on the core facts: a global outage, Outlook and Teams affected, elevated load, and no malicious activity.
Trust label: Accounts differ — Outlets agree on the outage itself, but the figures in their headlines and snippets differ (365 against 12).

Still uncertain
How much longer Outlook will be down is the question the reports cannot answer: no restoration time is given in the material reviewed. The full scale of the outage, how many users were affected and when each service came back are also unstated.
