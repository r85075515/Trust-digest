What happened
Lawyers for Reddit and the Digital Freedom Project told the High Court the ban robs children of their right to free communication. BBC News and Al Jazeera both describe the hearing as a bid to overturn the under-16s ban, with the challengers contending the restriction goes beyond what the constitution allows. Why it matters
The ruling will decide whether Australia can keep barring under-16s from social media in its current form. A decision upholding the ban would embolden similar proposals elsewhere, while a decision striking it down would force lawmakers back to narrower tools for protecting children online.
