10:T6bb,What happened
Hi Noon Hospitality announced plans to open B&R Fish Shop in December 2026 at 4343 E. Camelback Road in Phoenix, right next door to its original Buck & Rider restaurant near Camelback Road and 44th Street. Occupying a 4,500-square-foot former Panera Bread storefront, the concept will retail fresh daily seafood, hand-rolled sushi, sake, and wine.

Why it matters
In a landlocked desert metropolis like Phoenix, daily air-freighted seafood sourcing has long been a differentiator for Buck & Rider since opening in 2015. Extending that supply chain into a standalone retail fishmonger and commissary kitchen lets home cooks purchase restaurant-grade seafood directly while scaling prep capacity across Hi Noon Hospitality's restaurant group.

Source differences
KTAR ("Buck & Rider to sell fresh seafood at B&R Fish Shop in Phoenix") highlights the 4,500-square-foot market's retail offerings—including fresh seafood, hand-rolled sushi, wine, and sake—and notes an exact December opening date has not yet been announced. The AZ Insider ("Something Fishy Is Happening on Camelback - The AZ Insider") specifies the 4343 E. Camelback Road address in a former Panera space next to the original Buck & Rider. Heyman Luxury ("B&R Fish Shop on Camelback Road: Buck & Rider's Seafood Hub") adds that the facility will also cut and process seafood for every Hi Noon Hospitality location.

Still uncertain
The exact December grand-opening date, retail operating hours, and daily fish counter pricing remain unannounced.
